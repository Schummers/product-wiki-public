# AI as a Creative Teammate

## Summary

A 2025 study of 776 Procter & Gamble employees examined how AI affects collaborative ideation compared to individual work. Participants were assigned to work individually or in pairs, with or without AI assistance. The findings reveal that teams using AI generate the highest-quality solutions overall, particularly in the top-performing 10% of ideas, despite individuals with AI producing competitive average results.

The research challenges the assumption that AI-assisted individual work renders team collaboration obsolete. While individuals using AI can match or exceed the output quality of non-AI teams on average, AI-enhanced team collaboration produces a disproportionately high share of breakthrough ideas. This suggests AI functions best as a teammate rather than merely as a tool, requiring intentional facilitation approaches that differ from traditional workshops.

## Key Takeaways

- **Teams with AI outperform all conditions** — AI-assisted teams generated both the highest average quality ideas and a disproportionately high number of top 10% solutions, outperforming individuals with AI and teams without AI.
- **Individuals with AI compete well on average** — AI-assisted individuals produced higher-quality ideas on average than individuals or teams without AI, and matched the quantity and detail of AI teams.
- **AI is not a replacement for teams** — while individuals with AI perform strongly, organizations that implement all ideas should prioritize team-based AI ideation for breakthrough solutions.
- **AI requires different facilitation** — LLMs built for individual use need intentional facilitation approaches to leverage team dynamics and AI capabilities together.
- **Context matters for AI use** — AI works best for ideation where "truth" is not the primary criterion, unlike data analysis where hallucinations introduce unacceptable risks.
