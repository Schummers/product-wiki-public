# Mobile UX: Study Guide

## Summary

A comprehensive study guide compiling Nielsen Norman Group's most useful articles and videos on mobile user experience, organized into 15 sections covering general considerations, interaction design, navigation, content, onboarding, gestures, device-specific features, multitasking, responsive design, cultural differences, devices beyond smartphones, ecommerce, testing, and historical perspective. The guide provides a curated learning path from foundational concepts to specialized topics.

## Key Takeaways

- **Broad resource collection** — The guide curates 100+ resources organized into logical sections, serving as a comprehensive reference for learning mobile UX across all dimensions.
- **Foundational concepts critical** — General considerations about mobile's strengths and limitations, the importance of following UX guidelines on mobile, and understanding context form the foundation for all mobile design.
- **Navigation patterns central** — Mobile navigation (hamburger menus, bottom navigation, subnavigation) is core to mobile design; navigation patterns have standardized and are widely understood by users.
- **Content design differs on mobile** — Mobile requires different content strategies than desktop; information must be prioritized, secondary content deferred, and screen space utilized efficiently.
- **Interaction design considerations are extensive** — Touch targets, accordions, sliders, carousels, input fields, and gesture-based interactions require careful consideration specific to mobile constraints and capabilities.
- **Cultural and accessibility diversity matters** — Mobile design must account for cultural differences (WeChat in China, apps in India) and accessibility needs (screen readers, motor impairments).
