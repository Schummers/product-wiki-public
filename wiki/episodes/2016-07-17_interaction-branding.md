# The Impact of Interaction Design on Brand Perception

## Summary

Interaction design significantly influences how users perceive a brand's personality and attributes, yet this influence often receives less attention than visual design. Research by Tolstrup and Heyer measured relationships between specific interactivity attributes and brand perception, studying eight interactivity attributes: responsiveness, direct vs. indirect manipulation, precision, pliability, continuous vs. discrete behavior, clear labels, expected vs. unexpected behavior, and consistency. They found strong connections between interaction qualities and brand attributes like sincerity, competence, and sophistication, as well as emotional responses like joy and anger. Key findings reinforced best practices: clear labels and consistent, expected behavior associated with sincerity and competence, while inconsistent or unexpected behavior triggered surprise and anger. Designers should inventory their interaction-design attributes and deliberately consider how they support or detract from stated brand values.

## Key Takeaways

- **Interaction design shapes brand perception** — Almost every aspect of a digital interface—visual design, content design, and interaction design—influences brand perception, yet interaction design often receives less attention.
- **Clear labels convey sincerity and competence** — Prototypes with clear labels suggesting actions triggered feelings of joy and associations with sincerity and competence.
- **Consistency and expectedness matter** — Elements that functioned as expected or demonstrated consistency were associated with sincerity and competence; inconsistent or unexpected behavior triggered surprise and anger.
- **Caution with unexpected interactions** — While carefully designed unexpected interactions can trigger pleasant surprise, they can also make users feel powerless; testing with real users is essential.
- **Eight interactivity attributes** — Responsiveness, direct/indirect manipulation, precision, pliability, continuous/discrete behavior, clear labels, expected/unexpected behavior, and consistency all impact how users perceive brand attributes.

## Quotes

> When the prototypes functioned as users expected or demonstrated consistency, people associated them with sincerity and competence. On the other hand, prototypes that did not behave consistently or conform to expectations were, again, associated with surprise and anger.

> In interactive systems such as websites, the way people interact with the user interface obviously has a strong impact on their feelings about the system. We know that people prefer designs that are easy to use. But as this research shows, the impact of interaction design goes deeper than simple preferences or user satisfaction. Interactivity influences customers' perceptions of brand traits just like visuals and content do.

## Concepts

- [[Interaction Design]] — Interaction design is a primary vehicle for communicating brand attributes; responsiveness, consistency, and clear feedback signal competence and sincerity.
- [[Branding]] — Brand is expressed not just through visuals but through how interactive elements behave, respond, and communicate intent, shaping how users form impressions of brand personality; consistency and clarity in these interactions strengthen positive perceptions.
- [[Cognitive Psychology]] — Users associate interaction qualities like responsiveness and predictability with brand attributes like competence and sincerity.
- [[Emotional Design]] — Careful interaction design can evoke positive emotions like joy and pleasant surprise, while poor interaction design triggers frustration and anger.
