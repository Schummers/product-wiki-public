# The Product Triad: Design's Role

## Summary

The product triad (also called the product trio or three-legged-stool model) is a common organizational pattern in Agile teams composed of one representative each from design, product management, and engineering. Rooted in IDEO's DVF framework (Desirable, Viable, Feasible), the triad aims to ensure products address user needs, business viability, and technical feasibility. The article clarifies that effective triads dissolve siloed ownership rather than assigning each member responsibility for one aspect. For designers specifically, the role involves becoming the authority on user needs, ensuring the team prioritizes work that creates user value and, by extension, business value.

## Key Takeaways

- **Shared Ownership Over Silos** — The triad's purpose is not to have each member own one aspect of the DVF framework, but to collaborate jointly on achieving all three; one member may lead on decisions but ownership remains collective.
- **Design as User Advocate** — The designer's primary responsibility is articulating user problems and becoming the authority on user needs through research, ensuring the team prioritizes work that solves important user problems and creates user value.
- **Viability Through Design** — Design contributes to product viability by understanding business objectives, making informed design decisions, and exposing conflicts between stakeholder assumptions and business goals early through the design vision.
- **Feasibility Through Collaboration** — Involving the engineering lead directly in the design process ensures the final vision is clear and achievable; designers must also understand backend requirements and iterate with engineers as technical realities emerge.
- **Autonomy Through Expertise** — Designers demonstrate product-triad autonomy by using user-centered insights to drive stakeholder alignment rather than acting on assumptions, preventing teams from building the wrong thing.

## Quotes

> Effective triads collaborate on achieving all three aspects; while one individual may lead on ensuring that the necessary decisions get made, the team owns the decisions jointly.

> An effective product designer is key to the autonomy of the product triad.
