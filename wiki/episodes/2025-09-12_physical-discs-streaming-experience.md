# A Millennial's DVD Collection: I'm Returning to Physical Discs

## Summary

The author describes her shift from relying on streaming services back to collecting physical media after the streaming experience deteriorated. What was once a revolutionary improvement over Blockbuster has become a frustrating ecosystem of authentication issues, subscription tiers, forced ads, and unreliable access. The article explores why physical media's inconveniences now feel preferable to the complexity and poor design of modern streaming and smart TV interfaces.

## Key Takeaways

- **Convenience requires reliability** — A seamless experience that fails occasionally is less convenient than a slower but predictable method that always works without authentication or service interruptions.
- **Curated choices beat limitless options** — A carefully maintained collection of 200 personal favorites provides more satisfaction and discoverability than algorithmically recommended selections from 100,000+ options.
- **Smart TVs prioritize data collection over core function** — Devices marketed as "smart" often implement deceptive patterns, forced data collection, and mandatory consent, treating users as data sources rather than prioritizing their core viewing experience.
- **Cross-device complexity creates friction** — Each device requires separate sign-ins, password resets, app updates, and subscription management, making seamless multi-device experiences a promise rather than a reality.
- **Real-world use matters more than theoretical design** — Companies that fail to understand actual user behavior in messy real-life scenarios (like wanting to restart a show on a different device) inevitably fail to deliver meaningful value.

## Quotes

> "the streaming experience has become so atrocious that I — a person who grew up in the 90s — am back to collecting DVDs and Blu-rays."

> "the digital-streaming experience has become so bad, physical media is pleasant by comparison."

> "Convenience that isn't reliable isn't convenient. Physical media works every time I insert a disc. No authentication, no updates, no new ad tier."
