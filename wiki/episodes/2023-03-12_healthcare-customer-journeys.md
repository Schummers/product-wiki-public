# Digital Interactions in Healthcare Customer Journeys

## Summary

This article presents findings from a longitudinal diary study examining how patients interact with healthcare providers through digital channels following the pandemic's acceleration of telehealth adoption. Over one month, nine participants logged 93 healthcare interactions, revealing patterns about device preferences, interaction types, and channel choices. Key findings include that most journeys include at least one digital touchpoint, smartphones dominate for simple interactions, computers are chosen for complex tasks, and patient portals are the most-used digital channel. The research shows healthcare customers actively switch between devices and channels to optimize for convenience and functionality, challenging assumptions about single-channel design.

## Key Takeaways

- **Most healthcare journeys blend digital and in-person** — the majority of patient journeys (35 out of 53) include at least one digital touchpoint, with customers moving fluidly between channels throughout a single journey.
- **Smartphones dominate simple tasks** — 78 out of 93 interactions occurred on smartphones, primarily for scheduling and communication because mobile devices are convenient and accessible.
- **Computers handle complexity** — when tasks require intake forms, detailed information entry, or features missing on mobile sites, patients switch to computers for better functionality and reliability.
- **Patient portals are most-used channel** — 32 out of 93 interactions used patient portals because they are asynchronous, consolidate multiple functionalities, and eliminate phone tag.
- **Device choice reflects task efficiency** — patients choose devices and channels based on which option provides greatest ease of use or task efficiency, not loyalty to one platform.
- **Portal consolidation appeals to users** — having all relevant interactions (scheduling, messaging, results, video visits) in one place reduces friction and improves patient satisfaction.

## Concepts

- [[Customer Journey]] — healthcare customer journeys typically include multiple touchpoints across devices and channels, with patients switching between channels based on task complexity and device capabilities.
- [[Healthcare UX]] — effective healthcare digital experiences require supporting both simple interactions (scheduling, messaging) on mobile and complex tasks (intake forms) on desktop within a cohesive patient journey.
- [[Multi-channel Experience]] — patients expect to move seamlessly between channels and devices; patient portals that consolidate functionality across scheduling, messaging, and appointments perform best.
