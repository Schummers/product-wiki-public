# NN/g's Free UX Templates and Guides

## Summary

This article curates a collection of free NN/g templates and guides designed to accelerate UX work and product development. The templates cover the complete UX workflow, from discovery and research through design and team management. By providing pre-built templates for common UX tasks, this resource helps practitioners overcome creative blocks and reduce time spent on repetitive planning and documentation work.

## Key Takeaways

- **Discovery and research templates streamline planning** — templates for research plans, consent forms, interview guides, and usability testing procedures provide starting points that save time on documentation.
- **Workshop facilitation templates enable collaboration** — resources for facilitating workshops, defining team norms, and conducting activities like cognitive walkthroughs help teams work together more effectively.
- **Mapping templates surface user needs and processes** — pre-built templates for journey maps, service blueprints, empathy maps, and storyboards help teams visualize customer experiences and identify opportunities.
- **Content and design templates maintain consistency** — templates for content audits, error messages, design critiques, and visual design ensure quality and efficiency in design execution.
- **Team structure templates clarify roles** — RACI matrices and skill-mapping templates help organizations define responsibilities and identify training needs.
