# Planning Research with Generative AI

## Summary

AI chatbots can efficiently support UX researchers in planning studies by helping generate research questions, identify appropriate methods, create inclusion criteria, and develop study collateral. Rather than asking AI for a complete research plan (which produces generic templates), researchers should deconstruct the plan into components and tackle each separately. The article provides step-by-step prompting strategies, emphasizing the importance of providing context, understanding AI limitations (it suggests without domain expertise), reviewing outputs for common mistakes, and treating AI as a research assistant rather than a mentor.

## Key Takeaways

- **Componentized approach** — decompose research plans into individual parts (context, research questions, methods, criteria, screeners) and address each separately rather than requesting a complete plan at once.
- **Context provision** — start conversations by providing organizational context, product/service description, and learning objectives to ground AI recommendations in specific goals.
- **Research questions first** — generate 10+ research questions, filter duplicates offline, and refine before asking AI to suggest methods.
- **Method selection** — ask AI which methods suit which questions and why; generative AI may over-suggest triangulation or behavioral methods when attitudinal ones are appropriate.
- **Study collateral** — have AI create screeners, interview questions, and tasks while providing specific guidance to avoid common mistakes (priming, vague task instructions, poor screening distractors).

## Quotes
