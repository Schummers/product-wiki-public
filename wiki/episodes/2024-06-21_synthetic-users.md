# Synthetic Users: If, When, and How to Use AI-Generated "Research"

## Summary

Synthetic users are AI-generated profiles that simulate user groups through text-based interviews and surveys. While they can rapidly synthesize available data about new domains, they fundamentally cannot replace real user research. The authors tested synthetic users against real research and found them prone to sycophancy, idealization, oversimplification, and lack of contextual understanding. The article emphasizes that real-user research is essential; synthetic users can supplement research through hypothesis generation and desk work, but should never replace actual user engagement.

## Key Takeaways

- **Real user research is non-negotiable** — synthetic users cannot replace the depth, empathy, and behavior understanding gained from studying actual people.
- **Synthetic users have built-in bias toward agreement** — AI chatbots tend to be sycophantic, making them likely to approve of any concept and provide overly favorable feedback.
- **Synthetic users oversimplify human complexity** — they lack contextual understanding of why people make specific choices; responses are one-dimensional and miss nuance.
- **Synthetic users idealize rather than reflect reality** — when asked if they completed courses, synthetic users overoptimistically reported completion; real users honestly reported abandonment.
- **Synthetic users generate shallow needs** — AI can list many needs but without prioritization or depth; users struggle to act on long lists of equally-weighted factors.
- **Desk research is a valid use case** — synthetic users can effectively synthesize available data (academic literature, forums, product information) into digestible overviews for completely new domains.
- **Treat synthetic findings as hypotheses** — if used, synthetic user outputs should guide real-user research questions rather than inform final decisions.

## Quotes

> "Real user research is essential. Synthetic users cannot replace the depth and empathy gained from studying and speaking with real people."

> "UX research explores human behavior. We need to hear about real human experiences to understand why people make specific choices, what specific contextual factors influence behavior, what's important, and when."

> "Human behavior is complex and context-dependent. Synthetic users miss this complexity."

## Concepts

- [[Synthetic Users]] — AI-generated user profiles that can supplement but never replace real user research; most effective for hypothesis generation and desk research.
- [[AI in User Research]] — artificial intelligence applied to research has limitations related to sycophancy, oversimplification, and inability to model actual behavior; requires careful interpretation.
- [[Research Methods]] — genuine UX research must include real users to capture behavioral complexity, priorities, and contextual factors that AI cannot model.
- [[Generative AI]] — AI tools have uses in research preparation and hypothesis generation but are unsuitable for final decision-making without real-user validation.
- [[Research Limitations]] — synthetic users are limited by training data quality, sycophancy bias, inability to simulate behavior, and shallow understanding of priorities.
- [[AI and UX]] — AI can accelerate some UX activities but cannot replace human insight; responsible use of AI in UX requires clear understanding of its limitations.
