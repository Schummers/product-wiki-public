# Continuous Discovery Habits: Foreword: Marty Cagan

## Summary

Marty Cagan, author of *Inspired* and founder of the Silicon Valley Product Group, reflects on the scarcity of experienced product coaches. He describes how most teams struggle without hands-on coaching and explains why Teresa Torres's book is important for bridging this gap. The book provides the practical structure teams need to discover good opportunities and effective solutions.

## Key Takeaways

- **Coaching shortage** — Most product teams lack access to experienced leaders who can coach them in modern product practices, despite wanting to improve and understanding the theory.
- **Scalable solution** — Experienced product coaches provide the most scalable way to improve organizations, as hiring world-class internal leaders is difficult due to their high demand.
- **Teresa Torres's expertise** — Cagan has recommended Torres to many organizations worldwide and observed visible improvements in the teams she coached, validating her approach.
- **Structured guidance needed** — Many teams want to do great work but lack knowledge of what to do and when to do it, which is exactly what Torres's book provides.
- **Outcome-focused mindset** — The book teaches teams how to discover good opportunities and effective solutions while achieving necessary business outcomes, moving beyond vague best practices.

## Quotes

> "Most people are not as lucky as I was. Many work in organizations where the leaders have not had a chance to see how good product teams work up close."

> "I meet so many product teams that genuinely want to do great work, but they're just not sure what they need to do to get started solving real problems for their customers while achieving the necessary outcomes for their company."

> "My hope is that, if you haven't had the good fortune to be coached by a strong leader or product coach, this book can help fill that gap and set you on the path to success."

## Concepts

- [[Product Discovery]] — Cagan emphasizes that most teams lack the coaching and structure needed to practice effective product discovery.
- [[Product Outcome]] — The book teaches teams how to connect customer problem-solving to achieving business outcomes rather than building predetermined features.
- [[Stakeholder Engagement]] — Experienced coaching helps teams navigate the challenge of discovering solutions while meeting organizational needs.
