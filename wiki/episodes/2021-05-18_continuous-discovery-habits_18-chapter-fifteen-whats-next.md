# Continuous Discovery Habits: Chapter Fifteen: What's Next?

## Summary

The concluding chapter of *Continuous Discovery Habits* shifts from teaching discovery habits to supporting the reader's ongoing development. Rather than ending with a final lesson, Torres recognizes that translating book learning into workplace practice is challenging and offers a curated set of resources—from a monthly newsletter to membership communities, master classes, skills deep-dives, and one-on-one coaching—all designed to help readers continue their discovery practice in community with like-minded peers.

## Key Takeaways

- **Subscribe to the Product Talk newsletter** — monthly long-form articles share real-world teams practicing continuous discovery and provide actionable how-to guidance.
- **Join a community of practice** — the membership community provides monthly peer connection calls, fireside chats with successful practitioners, and access to curated "Worthy Reads" library.
- **Take specialized skills deep-dives** — offer targeted courses in story-based interviewing, opportunity mapping, and other specific discovery skills provide deliberate practice beyond the book.
- **Consider master classes for accountability** — live, small-group instruction creates space for hands-on application and peer learning.
- **Hire coaching for personalized guidance** — one-on-one coaching can help teams navigate the unique constraints of their organizational context.
- **Share your learning by reviewing the book** — readers' reviews help other product people decide whether the book will invest their time.

## Quotes

> We learn best in community. If you want to surround yourself with like-minded peers who are also investing in their continuous discovery habits, come join us.
