# Fresh Start Effect: How to Motivate Users with New Beginnings

## Summary

The fresh-start effect is a psychological phenomenon where temporal landmarks (new years, birthdays, seasons, job changes, holidays) trigger reflection and inspire people to pursue goals for better behavior. These landmarks create a "clean slate" feeling that separates past missteps from the untarnished present and future, making people more likely to initiate goal-oriented aspirational behaviors. Even the anticipation of a new decade (approaching 39, 49, etc.) can trigger meaning-seeking behaviors. Designers can ethically leverage this effect by pairing aspirational behavior messages with meaningful temporal landmarks, but should ensure the nudge benefits both the user and the organization.

## Key Takeaways

- **Definition and mechanism** — The fresh-start effect states that people are more likely to take action toward goals after temporal landmarks representing new beginnings; the effect works because landmarks create psychological separation from past failures.
- **Temporal landmarks vary in scope** — Landmarks range from weekly (Monday) to yearly (New Year) to personally meaningful (birthdays, job changes, holidays); the most significant effects come from occasions already psychologically meaningful to users.
- **Anticipatory effect** — The phenomenon occurs not just at landmarks but in anticipation of them; for example, people approaching the end of a decade in age engage in more meaning-seeking behaviors before the landmark arrives.
- **Three-step implementation** — Understand users' aspirational behaviors, connect them to company offerings, and pair messages with temporal landmarks; timing and relevance are critical to effectiveness.
- **Ethical use is essential** — The effect can be used to encourage beneficial behaviors (health, savings, learning), but using it to push destructive behaviors violates ethical principles; design should benefit both users and organizations.

## Quotes

> Temporal landmarks that signify a new time period—a fresh start– help us leave our missteps in the past and create an untarnished feeling for our present and future selves.

> Any time we manipulate designs to take advantage of what we know about human behavior, its imperative to do so ethically and responsibly.
