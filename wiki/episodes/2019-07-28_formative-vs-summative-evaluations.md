# Formative vs. Summative Evaluations

## Summary

Formative and summative evaluations serve different purposes in the design process. Formative evaluations identify aspects of a design that work well or poorly and why; they occur throughout redesigns and provide iterative feedback to incrementally improve interfaces. Summative evaluations assess how well a finished design performs, often compared to benchmarks (prior version, competitors, or industry standards); they occur less frequently, typically before or after major redesigns. Research questions determine evaluation type: "Does our navigation work?" (formative); "How does our app compare to competitors?" (summative). Contrary to common misconception, both evaluation types can be qualitative or quantitative; formative need not be qualitative (large companies may run quantitative formative studies), and summative can be qualitative (expert review). The ultimate summative evaluation is a go/no-go decision before launch: is the design good enough to release, or does it risk harming the brand?

## Key Takeaways

- **Formative Evaluations Guide Design Iteration** — formative evaluations identify what works and why during the design process, enabling incremental improvements; they are conducted repeatedly throughout redesigns with usually a few users per study.
- **Summative Evaluations Assess Final Performance** — summative evaluations evaluate finished products against benchmarks (prior versions, competitors, industry standards); they occur less frequently and provide big-picture usability assessments.
- **Research Question Determines Type** — formative questions ask "What works?" and "What breaks?" (guiding design); summative questions ask "How does this perform?" and "How does it compare?" (assessing outcomes).
- **Both Can Be Qualitative or Quantitative** — formative evaluation is often qualitative (cheaper, faster iteration) but can be quantitative for major features in high-maturity organizations; summative evaluations are often quantitative but can be qualitative (expert review).

## Quotes

> Formative evaluations focus on determining which aspects of the design work well or not, and why. These evaluations occur throughout a redesign and provide information to incrementally improve the interface.

> Summative evaluations occur less frequently than formative evaluations, usually right before or right after a redesign.

> If you truly embrace our proposition that brand is experience in the digital age, then consider a final summative evaluation before release.

## Concepts

- [[Formative Evaluation]] — iterative evaluation during design identifying what works and what breaks; provides feedback to guide incremental improvements; can be qualitative or quantitative.
- [[Summative Evaluation]] — evaluation of finished designs comparing performance against benchmarks (prior version, competitor, industry standard); assesses overall usability and informs go/no-go release decisions.
- [[Iterative Design]] — formative evaluations support iterative design cycles: prototype, test, identify issues, improve, and repeat; this cycle continues until the design is ready for release or summative evaluation.
