# Solving Product Design Exercises: Introduction

## Summary

This introduction frames the book's purpose: to address a gap in design education and professional practice. While the design industry is thriving and design roles are becoming more valued, most design schools and companies still focus primarily on aesthetics rather than business impact. The book aims to teach designers and organizations how design connects to business goals and value creation.

## Key Takeaways

- **Design industry evolution** — Designers are gaining influence and seat-at-table positions in companies and becoming founders themselves, but success requires understanding business impact beyond visual aesthetics.
- **Skills gap in design education** — Design schools teach visual skills but not the cross-functional abilities needed in tech companies: business understanding, working with engineers, presenting to non-designers, research, metrics optimization, and performance measurement.
- **Business-minded designers create value** — The most successful designers understand how design affects business KPIs and organizational strategy, not just how things look. This deeper impact is what drives career progression and company benefits.
- **Designers solving business problems** — Teaching designers to frame solutions around business problems would produce better products and help address urgent societal challenges.

## Quotes

> The most successful designers in our industry today have a deep understanding of how design affects business and its value within the organisation.

> Design schools often don't teach all the skills needed to become successful in design roles in tech companies: how design affects business; how to work with engineers; how to present your work to non-designers; how to research and understand customers; how to optimise design to deliver on the business' KPIs (Key Performance Indicators); how to measure a design's performance etc.

> I'm convinced that designers who learn how to help companies to solve business problems will help us to build better products and, hopefully, solve the most urgent problems the world is facing today, making it more sustainable, healthy and equal.
