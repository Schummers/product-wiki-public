# Four AI Superpowers: Where AI Improves Products

## Summary

This article identifies four primary areas where generative AI currently excels and delivers genuine user value: content creation, text summarization, basic data analysis, and perspective taking. The author emphasizes that product teams must start with a clear understanding of user problems and desired outcomes before deciding whether AI is an appropriate solution, rather than adopting AI for its own sake. Real examples from Bumble, Gmail, Mixpanel, and Final Round AI demonstrate effective AI implementation across these categories.

## Key Takeaways

- **Problem comes before technology** — product teams must first establish a research-backed understanding of user problems and clear desired outcomes; only then should they ask whether generative AI is the right solution for that specific problem.
- **AI has four primary superpowers** — generative AI currently excels at content creation, text summarization, basic data analysis, and perspective taking; these are the areas most likely to deliver genuine user value rather than features built for technology's sake.
- **Content creation is a natural fit** — products involving creation or modification of content (such as Bumble's Icebreaker feature for generating opening messages) benefit significantly from AI-enhanced capabilities because this aligns with LLM strengths.
- **AI addresses user friction** — summarization (like Gmail's email summaries), data analysis (such as Mixpanel's natural-language query features), and perspective taking (exemplified by Final Round AI's interview practice) solve concrete user problems by making complex tasks more accessible.
- **AI is one tool among many** — the most innovative products combine clear intention, thoughtful design, and AI only in scenarios where it adds genuine value; adopting AI without validating its relevance to user outcomes results in useless features regardless of technical sophistication.
