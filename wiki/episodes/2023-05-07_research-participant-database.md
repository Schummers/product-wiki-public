# Best Practices for Building and Maintaining Your Own Research-Participant Database

## Summary

Building an internal research-participant panel enables UX teams to conduct frequent studies efficiently and cost-effectively, though it requires significant upfront investment and ongoing maintenance. Teams must choose appropriate tools, decide what participant data to collect, track engagement history to avoid overusing participants, establish ethical data practices, populate the panel through strategic channels, assign ownership, and communicate clear governance rules. While internal panels carry risks like sampling bias toward loyal customers, the efficiency gains make them valuable for mature UX teams conducting regular research.

## Key Takeaways

- **Tool choice depends on team maturity** — Enterprise tools like Excel are simple but require manual work; CRM software adds structure; specialized tools streamline full workflows but cost more.
- **Collect only necessary data** — Keep demographic and qualification information focused; capturing too much requires ongoing maintenance and storage obligations without providing value.
- **Track engagement history carefully** — Record last contact, participation dates, articulation level, and study count to avoid over-recruiting participants and to identify good candidates for future studies.
- **Establish strong data governance** — Work with legal and compliance teams on security policies, storage methods, and GDPR compliance; communicate policies clearly to opt-in participants.
- **Populate strategically from multiple channels** — Use existing customer touchpoints like support interactions, surveys, events, and social media to recruit panel members.
- **Audit regularly and allow opt-out** — Remove outdated contacts and disengaged participants; ensure panel members can easily opt out whenever contacted.

## Quotes

> An internal participant panel allows researchers to be nimble with the research they do, spinning up small studies when they need quick insights.

> Remember, your panel may be skewed toward loyal customers and fans, so its use could introduce bias.
