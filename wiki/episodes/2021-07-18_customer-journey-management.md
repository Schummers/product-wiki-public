# The Practice of Customer-Journey Management

## Summary

Journey management is the ongoing practice of researching, measuring, optimizing, and orchestrating a customer journey to improve the experience and achieve business goals. It represents a natural evolution from interaction-level UX work to journey-level customer experience, addressing the full end-to-end process customers go through to complete a goal. Organizations increasingly recognize that good customer journey experiences translate to increased satisfaction, better business outcomes (revenue, churn, repeat purchases), and that dedicated roles and resources are required to manage this complexity.

## Key Takeaways

- **Beyond Interaction Design** — Journey management extends UX practice beyond single touchpoints to the complete customer experience across multiple channels; it requires applying user-centered design principles to the journey-level experience, not just isolated interactions.
- **Three Core Competencies** — Journey management comprises insights (research and data collection), design (strategic UX work and ideation), and orchestration (personalized, real-time delivery using behavioral data and specialized tools).
- **Cross-Functional Nature** — Customer journeys span multiple organizational departments and channels; journey management requires intentional collaboration, alignment, and integration of disparate systems to create a unified view of customer data.
- **Dedicated Roles and Teams** — Effective journey management requires dedicated practitioners who own a single customer journey strategically; these roles must sit relatively high in the organizational structure to influence siloed teams.
- **Measurement and Optimization** — Journey management involves establishing clear journey-level goals, tracking journey performance metrics, and continuously iterating to meet customer needs while achieving business objectives.

## Quotes

## Concepts

- [[Customer Journey]] — A customer journey is the end-to-end process a customer goes through to complete a task, spanning multiple touchpoints, channels, and organizational departments; journey management treats the full journey as a strategic entity requiring coordinated research, design, and optimization.
- [[Journey Management]] — This practice systematically researches, measures, optimizes, and orchestrates customer journeys; it evolved from interaction-level UX to address how organizations can deliver seamless, personalized experiences across channels.
- [[Journey Orchestration]] — Orchestration is the delivery of personalized journeys by tracking behavioral data to understand customer needs and deliver the right interactions on the right channel at the right time, often through specialized tools and AI/ML systems.
