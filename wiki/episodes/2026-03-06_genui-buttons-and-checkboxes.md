# The Most Exciting Development in GenUI: Buttons and Checkboxes

## Summary

Real generative UI—where AI systems decide in real time to generate interactive elements customized to individual users—is beginning to appear in AI chat interfaces. Simple form elements like buttons, checkboxes, and multi-select fields are now being contextually generated by AI when the system determines they would improve the experience. This represents progress toward AI systems that generate interfaces rather than just text, with the most notable examples appearing in Google AI Mode and Claude's AskUserQuestion widget.

## Key Takeaways

- **GenUI in chat** — The most tangible progress toward true generative UI is happening within AI chat interfaces, where simple interactive elements appear contextually within conversations.
- **Friction reduction** — Checkboxes and form fields solve the real problem of reducing unnecessary friction in multi-step interactions; they allow selection without retyping information.
- **Context critical** — GenUI succeeds when it gathers needed clarification through interactive elements rather than requiring users to compose complex prompts upfront.
- **Design guardrails necessary** — Systems like Claude's AskUserQuestion use predesigned, constrained modules (limiting to four questions max) to ensure consistency rather than letting AI generate arbitrary interfaces.
- **Broad audience impact** — While power users experiment with more complex genUI, AI chat is where most people interact with AI now, so even small interaction improvements reach a massive audience.

## Quotes

> "Checkboxes are useful here in the same way they've always been useful in classic graphic user interfaces — they allow the user to directly select specific options, rather than typing them out."

> "It was never realistic to expect consumers to become perfect prompt engineers, comprehensively planning out the necessary context they'll have to provide upfront to achieve their goals."
