# Just Enough Research: Foreword

## Summary

Written by Jeffrey Zeldman, the foreword frames the book's purpose: teaching practical research methods for designers and developers working in constrained real-world environments. Zeldman acknowledges that research budgets are limited, schedules are tight, and organizational support for research is often uncertain. He argues that the book enables readers to "quickly ascertain key facts and gather important insights," helping teams design for actual users rather than assumptions, and converting skepticism about research into collaborative advocacy.

## Key Takeaways

- **Real-world constraints** — The foreword, written by Zeldman, frames research as solving for budgets that are constrained, schedules that are absurd, and organizations that may value algorithms over human insight rather than perfect research conditions.
- **Research as internal leverage** — Understanding real user needs and conducting research helps teams "head off or win the internal debates that so often send promising projects plummeting to perdition," converting antagonists into collaborative partners.
- **Speed and insight** — Zeldman emphasizes that research can deliver real benefits quickly, enabling teams to design the right experience for actual users rather than relying on personal peccadillos or marketing directives.

## Quotes

> In our world, budgets are constrained, schedules are absurd, there is little internal agreement about what constitutes worthwhile research, and "I don't like yellow" is considered feedback.

> Armed with Just Enough Research, you'll be able to quickly ascertain key facts and gather important insights, enabling you to design the right experience for the real, living people who actually use your website.

> Your team will never be the same again. It will be better.
