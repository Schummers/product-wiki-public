# Product & UX Glossary

## Summary

Sara Paul compiles a comprehensive glossary of product and business terminology that UX professionals need to understand to communicate effectively with product managers and business stakeholders. The glossary covers business models and revenue strategies (subscription, freemium, advertisement-based), strategic frameworks (blue-ocean strategy, SWOT analysis, business-model canvas), product lifecycle stages (introduction, growth, maturity, decline), key metrics (customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, product-market fit), and organizational practices (OKRs, RACI, feature flags, hypothesis-driven development). By learning this shared vocabulary, UX professionals can enter strategic conversations with business credibility and influence organizational decisions.

## Key Takeaways

- **Revenue models shape product strategy** — understanding subscription, freemium, and advertisement-based models helps UX professionals align their design work with business goals.
- **Product lifecycle stages require different approaches** — products move through introduction, growth, maturity, and decline stages, each with different strategic priorities and design challenges.
- **Key business metrics measure value creation** — customer acquisition cost (CAC), customer lifetime value (CLV), and the CAC/CLV ratio reveal whether customer segments are profitable.
- **Product-market fit indicates success** — when a product delivers ongoing value to users and the business, sustaining and scaling growth becomes possible; achieving this fit requires iterative refinement based on user feedback.
- **Strategic frameworks guide decision-making** — frameworks like blue-ocean strategy, SWOT analysis, and business-model canvas provide structured approaches to strategic thinking about markets, competition, and value.
- **Organizational practices enable execution** — OKRs align teams around measurable outcomes; RACI matrices clarify roles; feature flags enable safe experimentation; hypothesis-driven development reduces risk.
- **UX connects to business outcomes** — understanding product strategy, business metrics, and organizational practices allows UX professionals to position their work as a business driver rather than a compliance requirement.

## Quotes

> When UX professionals speak the language and understand the business roles of product managers, they can confidently enter strategic dialogues on equal footing. Using the same vernacular as a product manager is one of the most effective ways to build competence, trust, and influence.

> The following glossary includes relevant terms product managers often use to describe the inner workings of an organization, strategies, product development processes, and issues.

## Concepts

- [[Product Strategy]] — the high-level approach and decisions that guide how a product is developed, positioned, and evolved to achieve business and user goals.
- [[Business Metrics]] — quantitative measures used to evaluate whether a business and its products are achieving desired outcomes, including revenue, customer acquisition, retention, and profitability.
- [[Product Management]] — the discipline of guiding a product through its lifecycle, defining strategy, prioritizing work, and ensuring the product delivers value to both users and the business.
