# Collaborative Agile Activities Reduce Silos and Align Perspectives

## Summary

Collaborative ideation activities like 6-up 1-up, whiteboard sessions, and telephone sketching bring development teams into UX design processes, reducing designer workload while introducing diverse perspectives and improving buy-in. These lightweight activities work within Agile constraints, require minimal resources, and can be integrated into existing ceremonies like standups or planning sessions. Success depends on matching activity timing to task urgency: focused one-on-one work for immediate needs, larger group brainstorms for future priorities.

## Key Takeaways

- **6-up 1-up method** — individual 5-minute sketching of 6 rapid ideas, group discussion of themes, individual 5-minute refinement of one selected solution, final group review and hybrid selection; effective for larger teams and backlog-distant features.
- **Whiteboard sessions** — informal real-time collaboration with clear goals and acceptance criteria; effective for small teams and near-term sprints requiring immediate alignment between design, development, and product ownership.
- **Telephone activity** — sequential passing of a design sketch, each person adding ideas for 1-2 minutes before passing; generates diverse ideas and builds team comfort with iteration and building on others' work.
- **Timing based on urgency** — use focused one-on-one design for current sprint issues with developers; schedule larger brainstorms during lulls for features further down the backlog; scale involvement inversely to urgency.
- **Organizational integration** — introduce activities incrementally by carving time from existing ceremonies; customize methods to team needs; iterate on the process until it fits team workflows naturally.

## Quotes

> If you find yourself dreading your UX to-do list, consider bringing other members of your development team into your design process. Doing so not only will lighten your workload, but it will also introduce multiple perspectives and ease further collaboration processes (for example, if you need signoff from other departments).

> It takes a good amount of collaboration to have a smooth UX process on Agile teams.

> Patience is key as you introduce new techniques and activities to your team, but the end result will be a faster design process that involves fewer handoffs and silos because everyone is involved earlier.

## Concepts

- [[Agile Design]] — UX practice within Agile software development; requires adapting design activities to sprint cadences, reducing upfront specification, and supporting rapid iteration.
- [[Ideation]] — the creative process of generating ideas; collaborative ideation harnesses team diversity while building shared understanding of design solutions.
- [[Team Collaboration]] — working across roles to improve outcomes; involving developers and product managers in design generates better solutions and reduces handoff friction.
