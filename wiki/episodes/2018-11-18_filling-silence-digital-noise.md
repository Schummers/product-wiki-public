# Filling the Silence with Digital Noise

## Summary

This article examines how people use digital media and devices to fill silent moments in their lives, both literally through background media and figuratively by avoiding boredom or downtime. Field and diary studies reveal that participants often play television, music, or video content in the background while performing other activities, and compulsively reach for smartphones during waiting periods. While some users don't express concern about this behavior, many acknowledge feeling trapped by the compulsion to constantly engage with digital content, linking it to the broader Vortex phenomenon of feeling out of control online.

## Key Takeaways

- **Digital media serves as background noise to avoid silence** — Participants turn on television, music, or podcasts while doing other activities, often ignoring the content but needing its presence to avoid silence and maintaining a compulsive need for audio stimulation.

- **Killing time is a primary device use pattern** — Rather than being bored during waiting periods, users immediately reach for phones and tablets to engage with content, even when the content doesn't matter as much as the act of doing something.

- **Compulsive device use stems from discomfort with silence** — Some participants explicitly reported needing background noise or mental stimulation, suggesting an underlying anxiety about silence that design capitalizes on.

- **Multitasking with media is common across cultures** — Field studies in the US, China, and other locations found similar patterns of using devices to fill empty moments, suggesting universal human inclinations toward engagement.

- **Concern about overuse exists but is not universal** — While many expressed worry and frustration about device usage, others felt in control or didn't mind spending time on devices, indicating varying attitudes toward digital consumption.

- **Design intentionally facilitates the silence-filling behavior** — Tech companies benefit from users filling time and have designed products with sophisticated techniques to encourage and sustain this behavior.

## Quotes

> digital "noise" is used to fill the "silent" moments in people's lives both literally and figuratively:

> I don't like working in silence. I need to have the news on, or music. Background noise helps me.

> I always feel like I should be doing something with my hands.

- [[Digital Media]] — the role of streaming video, music, and content platforms in providing always-available material to fill perceived empty moments.

- [[Boredom]] — how discomfort with boredom drives frequent device checking and engagement, and how design leverages this psychological state.
