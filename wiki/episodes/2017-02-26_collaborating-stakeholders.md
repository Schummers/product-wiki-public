# How to Collaborate with Stakeholders in UX Research

## Summary

Involving stakeholders in UX research and design improves outcomes by building buy-in, creating shared ownership, encouraging empathy for users, and accelerating problem-solving. Stakeholder participation removes obstacles to future research, demonstrates UX value, and reduces time spent explaining methods and findings. The article provides strategies for motivating participation, making collaboration easy, and structuring activities so everyone contributes and feels valued.

## Key Takeaways

- **Stakeholder participation builds organizational support** — involvement demonstrates UX value, shows how early testing prevents costly downstream problems, and creates group ownership of usability outcomes.
- **Evangelize UX research to motivate participation** — explain how research reduces risk, aligns products with user expectations, and saves developer rework; position UX as foundational, not superficial.
- **Make time and space for collaboration** — schedule UX office hours, design clinics, and open chat channels; invite all roles to use UX as a resource; create physical or virtual spaces for interaction.
- **Educate stakeholders about participation benefits** — teach management how stakeholder involvement improves decisions; find champions who share participation stories.
- **Align research goals with stakeholder goals** — run strategy meetings to understand their objectives; tie UX success to goals that matter to them; give credit for successes.
- **Demonstrate utility with small pilots** — start with easy, vital opportunities; show quick wins and user insights; build momentum for larger efforts.
- **Make participation logistically easy** — schedule key activities when stakeholders can attend; personally invite each person; emphasize their importance to good outcomes.
- **Create accessible deliverables** — annotate prototypes; share data and insights via lightweight formats (bullet lists, screenshots); tell compelling stories; avoid dense academic-style reports.
