# The 4 Stages of AI Image Generation: An Experience Map

## Summary

Through contextual inquiry with Midjourney users, the authors identified four distinct stages in the AI image-generation workflow: Define (establishing goals and approach), Explore (creating many variations to find promising directions), Refine (making targeted adjustments to reach final output), and Export (adding finishing touches and upscaling). Users with different goals (inspiration-oriented vs. deliverable-oriented) move through these stages differently, spending more or less time in refinement depending on whether the output is meant for exploration or final delivery.

## Key Takeaways

- **Four stages structure the AI image-generation workflow** — Define, Explore, Refine, Export occur in sequence, though users may skip stages depending on their goal.
- **Goal type drives time allocation** — inspiration-oriented users (seeking concepts) spend more time exploring and may skip refinement; deliverable-oriented users spend most time refining toward perfection.
- **Define stage involves goal-setting and prompt research** — users decide if they want inspiration or final deliverables, then reference past images, chatbots, or external galleries for prompt ideas.
- **Explore stage emphasizes quantity over quality** — users generate many images (20-80) using strategies like prompt repetition or variation to increase likelihood of finding compelling directions.
- **Refine stage is difficult and frustrating** — users struggle with lack of control, fighting the AI to make small adjustments; many abandon refinement and finish in external tools (Photoshop, upscalers).
- **Export stage adds final touches** — after reaching refinement limits in the generation tool, users export and use other tools for finishing edits, upscaling, and text overlay.

## Quotes

> "One study participant wanted to create an image of a steampunk-themed sofa. After multiple iterations, she got an image of a sofa she was happy with but wanted to change the upholstery from plain to striped. This edit proved difficult, as the appearance of the sofa would change every time she tried to make an adjustment, due to the randomness of the AI tool."

> "Discovering alternatives superior to their original vision was the major benefit of AI image generation."

## Concepts

- [[Image Generation]] — users move through predictable stages when generating images with AI, with distinct challenges and strategies at each stage.
- [[Generative AI]] — AI image generation tools enable rapid exploration of visual concepts but lack fine-grained control, forcing users to choose between iteration or external finishing.
- [[User Research]] — contextual inquiry revealed user needs and pain points in AI image generation, highlighting opportunities for tool improvement (especially in refinement).
- [[Creative Process]] — the four-stage model maps the creative workflow, showing how users shift between exploration (quantity), refinement (quality), and finishing.
- [[AI in Design]] — AI generation tools are most powerful for exploring many directions quickly; they are less effective for precise control and fine adjustments.
- [[Experience Mapping]] — mapping the user journey through image generation reveals bottlenecks and opportunities for tool improvement at each stage.
