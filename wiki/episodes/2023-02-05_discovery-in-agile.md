# Discovery in Agile

## Summary

Discovery, the process of learning about problems before developing solutions, is essential to Agile but often rushed or skipped due to tight sprint timelines. Rather than fitting all discovery into a single sprint, the healthier approach is to scale discovery to fit available time and resources while maintaining continuous learning. The article presents the SCALE mnemonic for managing discovery in Agile: Speak and spike, Capture, Assign, Learn and share, Evaluate and decide.

## Key Takeaways

- **Scale, don't skip** — Discovery timing should not be arbitrarily constrained to sprint length; scaled discovery focuses on specific, valuable activities and assumptions rather than every possible question.
- **SCALE mnemonic guides Agile discovery** — Speak and spike (communicate needs, use spikes for research), Capture (identify aligned and conflicted areas), Assign (distribute activities), Learn and share (communicate findings continuously), Evaluate and decide (assess readiness to move forward).
- **Spikes for time-boxed research** — Spikes add dedicated sprint time for user research; constrain to 1-2 key questions and complete early in the sprint to inform subsequent delivery work.
- **Problem statements align team perspective** — Have team members individually draft problem/opportunity statements, then converge to discuss and resolve misalignments; surfaces diverse perspectives and knowledge gaps.
- **Prioritize discovery activities** — Discovery includes more than user research (business docs, stakeholder interviews, analytics, past research, heuristic evaluation); prioritize the most valuable activities for the time available.
- **Cross-functional involvement** — UX, product, and engineers should participate in discovery; shared understanding from collaborative discovery speeds delivery and reduces rework.
- **Supporting factors for discovery in Agile** — UX representation in leadership, tracking discovery velocity, and avoiding premature delivery-task assignment help sustain discovery practice in Agile environments.

## Quotes

> "Agile isn't about moving fast or recklessly: it's about prioritizing and delivering small, high-value increments to users, early and often."

> "A healthy mindset for discovery in Agile is to scale it, not skip it."

- [[User Research]] — User research is one component of Agile discovery, but not the only one; secondary research, stakeholder interviews, and analytics often provide sufficient insight within sprint timeframes.
