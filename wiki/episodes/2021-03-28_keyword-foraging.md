# The Search Before the Search: Keyword Foraging

## Summary

Keyword foraging is a preliminary search users conduct to determine the right terminology for their actual query when they do not know what something is called. Users engage in keyword foraging when they know what an item looks like but not its name, need a solution but not the tool name, cannot remember terminology they once knew, or know a term in one language but not another. This behavior is common in specialized or complex domains like fashion, home improvement, or car parts. Search interfaces fail users when they cannot formulate accurate queries due to vocabulary gaps. Sites can help by using faceted navigation to teach domain vocabulary, accepting imperfect query formulations, using plain language in content, and avoiding invented terminology that users cannot discover.

## Key Takeaways

- **Users search to discover terminology** — Keyword foraging is the preliminary search phase users conduct before their actual search; they try various descriptions hoping to land on the correct term.
- **Occurs in unfamiliar domains** — Keyword foraging is most common when users are unfamiliar with the search domain, which includes specialized topics like fashion, car parts, skin care, or any complex subject area.
- **Faceted navigation teaches vocabulary** — Filter options and faceted systems on ecommerce sites expose users to domain terminology they need; seeing "dual-fuel range" as a filter option teaches users the correct term.
- **Avoid invented terminology** — Branded or invented terms create barriers; if you invent "SmartClean Technology" instead of using the established term "self-cleaning," users won't search for it and won't find your product.
- **Use plain language alongside branded terms** — When branded terminology is necessary, include the plain-language alternative nearby for both SEO benefits and to educate customers about what the product actually does.
- **Check search logs for foraging patterns** — Site search logs reveal the vocabulary users attempt and the gaps between what they search for and what the correct term is, providing direct evidence of foraging.
