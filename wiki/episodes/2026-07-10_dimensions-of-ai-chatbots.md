# The 5 Qualities of Site-Specific AI Chatbots

## Summary

This article identifies five key design qualities for site-specific AI chatbots: handoff willingness, flexibility, proactivity, emotional responsiveness, and transparency. These qualities guide early design decisions and provide a framework for evaluating existing chatbots. The article recognizes that users are still forming mental models of site-specific AI chatbots and deciding whether to view them as human customer-support representatives or frontier LLMs. Getting these qualities wrong can lead to user abandonment. Each quality is explored with examples of strong and weak implementations, illustrating how design choices impact user perception and satisfaction.

## Key Takeaways

- **Handoff willingness — never gatekeep when users ask for a human** — Users do not yet view AI chatbots as equivalent to humans. If a customer asks to speak with a human agent, the bot should immediately honor that request. Escalate proactively when the bot cannot answer effectively or when the user shows signs of frustration.
- **Flexibility — handle adjacent questions and adapt to user direction** — Bots that handle questions adjacent to their domain (e.g., dietary substitutions for a meal-kit service) feel far more useful than expanded FAQs. When users ask out-of-scope questions, acknowledge the limitation and redirect to what the bot can help with.
- **Proactivity — anticipate needs and suggest next steps, but stay focused** — Clarification proactivity asks for more information to tailor responses to ambiguous queries. Directional proactivity surfaces follow-up questions and related information after the primary goal is addressed, with direct links rather than just names.
- **Emotional responsiveness — acknowledge situations without pretending to feel or assuming emotions** — Recognize the user's situation (e.g., "Two weeks is a long time to wait") rather than expressing false feelings (e.g., "I'm sincerely sorry"). Do not assume or describe the user's inner state. Emotional responsiveness does not substitute for quick resolution.
- **Transparency — communicate identity, capability, limitations, and data practices** — Upfront disclosure that the bot is AI builds trust. Surface capabilities contextually when relevant, not as an exhaustive list. Explain why the bot cannot help and what it can do instead. Explain data practices in the conversation, not just privacy policies.

## Concepts

- [[AI Chatbots]] — require five key qualities—handoff willingness, flexibility, proactivity, emotional responsiveness, and transparency—to earn and maintain user trust.
- [[Conversational Design]] — must calibrate clarity of capabilities, emotional tone, and escalation paths so users understand what the bot can do and when to ask for human help.
- [[User Experience]] — is shaped by whether the chatbot respects user choice, adapts to their needs, acknowledges their situation, and communicates honestly about its limitations.
