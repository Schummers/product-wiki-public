# UX-Maturity Stage 5: Integrated

## Summary

Stage 5 is the highest level of UX maturity most organizations will reach. At this stage, UX is integrated throughout the organization and people across departments understand users and prioritize their needs. Most teams perform efficiently and effectively. UX practices are established and sometimes innovative, with contributions to the field itself. High awareness and buy-in for UX activities exists across the organization. Design systems and research operations are strong and well-managed. UX performance metrics are present in all employees' goals. While stage 5 represents successful and sustainable UX practice, it differs from the rarely-attained stage 6 in that business metrics drive UX priorities rather than pure user-centeredness driving the business.

## Key Takeaways

- **Everybody understands and values UX** — UX is understood, appreciated, and supported by leadership and teams across the organization. There is widespread respect and proactive support rather than resistance.
- **Process includes established and innovative methods** — Organizations use research, design, and content methods appropriately throughout the product lifecycle. Discoveries happen at project starts and iterative design is the norm.
- **Design systems and operations are robust** — Design systems, research repositories, and operational frameworks are managed by dedicated teams. These systems increase predictability and free practitioners to think strategically.
- **Metrics align with UX goals** — Metrics for success are based on business goals and often (though not always) driven by user-centered priorities. All teams employ user-centered metrics that are regularly monitored and updated.
- **Stage 5 is sustainable but stage 6 is difficult** — While stage 5 can be maintained long-term through systems and infrastructure, stage 6 (where users come first) is much harder to sustain and usually doesn't last in organizations.

## Quotes

> Stage 5 is a great place to be in terms of UX maturity. Organizations at stage 5 have successful and sustainable UX practices. Most teams at stage-5 organizations are performing efficiently and effectively and have a strong focus on users.

> Everybody in stage-5 organizations understands, appreciates, and has a desire to learn more about UX. A widespread respect for UX brings proactive support and involvement from all or most teams and department leaders and championing for the user by many people.

> Stage 5 is a perfectly great stage at which to operate long term.

## Concepts

- [[UX Maturity]] — The article describes stage 5 as the highest UX maturity level most organizations achieve, within a six-stage model.
- [[Organizational Integration]] — At stage 5, UX is fully integrated throughout the organization with understanding and support from all departments and levels.
- [[DesignOps]] — Stage 5 relies on strong design operations including design systems, research repositories, and dedicated ops teams to support scalable UX practice.
- [[Strategic UX Leadership]] — UX leaders work in conjunction with other organizational leaders and shape strategy, though business metrics ultimately remain the driving force rather than pure user-centeredness.
