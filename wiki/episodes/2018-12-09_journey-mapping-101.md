# Journey Mapping 101

## Summary

This article provides foundational knowledge on journey mapping, a core UX research and design method that visualizes the process a person goes through to accomplish a goal. Journey maps compile user actions, thoughts, and emotions into a narrative timeline, organized by journey phases. The article covers essential components (actor, scenario, phases, actions/mindsets/emotions, and opportunities), distinguishes journey maps from related methods like experience maps and service blueprints, and explains why journey mapping forces organizational alignment and shared understanding.

## Key Takeaways

- **Journey map definition and components** — A journey map visualizes a specific user's goal-oriented journey through high-level phases, documenting actions, thoughts, and emotions while identifying opportunities for improvement. Five key components are required: actor (persona), scenario with expectations, journey phases, actions/mindsets/emotions, and opportunities.

- **Actor and scenario focus provide clear perspective** — Each journey map should focus on a single persona and specific scenario to build a strong, clear narrative. Different user types or scenarios require separate maps to capture distinct viewpoints.

- **Journey phases organize the narrative** — Journey phases provide high-level structure (e.g., discover, try, buy, use, seek support for ecommerce), with actual phases determined by research and organizational data rather than guessed structure.

- **Journey maps force conversation and alignment** — The mapping process compels teams to establish a shared mental model of user experience, addressing the widespread problem of siloed success metrics and fragmented understanding across departments.

- **Opportunities translate insights to action** — The opportunities section (with context, ownership, and metrics) converts journey mapping insights into actionable knowledge, answering what must be done, who owns changes, and how improvements will be measured.

## Quotes

> "A journey map is a visualization of the process that a person goes through in order to accomplish a goal."

> "The shared artifact resulting from the mapping can be used to communicate an understanding of your user or service to all involved. Journey maps are effective mechanisms for conveying information in a way that is memorable, concise, and that creates a shared vision."

> "Journey mapping is a process that provides a holistic view of the customer experience by uncovering moments of both frustration and delight throughout a series of interactions."

## Concepts

- [[Journey Mapping]] — A visualization method documenting a specific persona's goal-oriented journey through phases, with documented actions, thoughts, emotions, and identified improvement opportunities.

- [[User Research]] — Journey mapping applies user research findings to create shared organizational understanding of customer experience and pain points.

- [[Service Design]] — Service blueprints extend journey maps by documenting the internal service components and processes required to support the customer journey shown in the journey map.
