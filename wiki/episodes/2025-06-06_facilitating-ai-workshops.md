# Facilitating AI-Enhanced Workshops: From Ideation to Action

## Summary

During an AI-enhanced workshop, facilitators must guide the ideation process differently than in traditional human-only sessions. The key difference: AI can generate hundreds of ideas in minutes, making the standard diverge-converge structure unmanageable. Facilitators need to modify the process by having AI narrow ideas before group convergence, prepare evaluation criteria, allocate adequate time for reading and iteration, encourage co-writing of prompts, and document digitally for easy reference.

The dynamics of human versus AI ideation differ fundamentally. Humans fixate on early ideas and converge toward group consensus, while AI generates diverse options rapidly. Facilitators must structure workshops to capture AI's strengths while preserving human judgment and group dynamics.

## Key Takeaways

- **Modify diverge-converge structure** — add an intermediate narrowing step where AI ranks ideas against evaluation criteria before group discussion, managing the overflow of AI-generated options.
- **Prepare evaluation criteria** — collaborate with the group to establish scoring frameworks (feasibility, desirability, viability, or custom criteria) that AI uses to prioritize ideas for discussion.
- **Allocate more time than expected** — despite AI's speed, participants need 8-10 minutes per prompt (compared to 5 minutes without AI) to write thoughtful prompts, read responses, and iterate.
- **Enable prompt co-writing** — allow small groups to collaborate on prompts and explore outputs together, generating discussion and alignment alongside ideation.
- **Use digital documentation** — shared documents, slides, or whiteboards accommodate AI's longer outputs better than physical sticky notes and enable easy copy-pasting.

## Concepts

- [[Workshop Facilitation]] — techniques for guiding productive group sessions, adapted for AI-enhanced collaboration.
- [[Diverge-Converge Process]] — ideation structure where teams generate many options (diverge) then discuss and prioritize (converge), modified for AI-generated volumes.
- [[Ideation]] — collaborative brainstorming sessions where AI generates idea volumes that human facilitators structure for evaluation.
- [[Prompt Engineering]] — the collaborative refinement of AI prompts to improve outputs, a process requiring adequate time and group discussion.
