# Nielsen Norman Group: 20 Years

## Summary

This milestone article documents Nielsen Norman Group's twenty-year journey from its founding in 1998 through 2018, highlighting the explosive growth of the UX field and the organization's contributions to advancing user-centered design. The article provides year-by-year accounts of major research initiatives, publications, and popular articles, along with quantitative metrics showing the organization's extensive output: 1,139 articles, 230 million page views, 10,000 test users observed, and training of 44,283 UX professionals.

## Key Takeaways

- **Exponential field growth** — the UX field has grown approximately 55 times larger since NN/g's founding, with requirements for good design increasing significantly across ecommerce, mobile, and other domains.
- **Independent research model** — NN/g's self-funded approach has enabled independence and objectivity, allowing unbiased research not driven by client interests or proprietary solutions.
- **Guiding principles** — early articles on fundamental topics like usability heuristics, homepage design, and web writing have proven remarkably durable and continue to be widely read decades later.
- **Evolution of focus** — during NN/g's first decade, most popular articles addressed web design; recently, focus has shifted toward user-centered design methods and processes.
- **Continuous improvement in web UX** — website task success rates improved from 66% in 2004 to 82% in 2016, demonstrating measurable progress in the field at a rate of 1.3 percentage points per year.

## Quotes

> The UX field is now 55 times bigger than when NN/g started — and we trained many of the people who entered UX during this period.

> Whatever we say is what we believe is right, based on 20 years of observing users.

> By helping hundreds of thousands of designers create better products, we have helped make billions of normal people less frustrated by computers and more empowered by technology.
