# How AI Succeeds (and Fails) to Help People Find Information

## Summary

Generative AI can help people overcome keyword foraging, the search-before-the-search required when users don't know terminology to describe what they need. AI chatbots and AI-powered search accept wordy, complex questions without requiring proper terminology, enabling users to articulate needs in full sentences. However, AI's capability to help is still limited by two barriers: users are often unaware of AI's full potential for information-seeking, and many systems fail to ask clarifying questions that would refine results. A detailed case study shows a user struggling to find plumbing repair information who eventually benefited from Gemini's responses and search-keyword suggestions, but faced frustration because the system didn't ask clarifying questions upfront or provide direct links to relevant videos. This reveals a critical discoverability barrier: users don't know what questions to ask or how to use AI fully.

## Key Takeaways

- **Keyword Foraging Still Exists** — Despite AI's capabilities, keyword foraging (the preliminary search to find the right terminology) remains a persistent barrier for users who don't know the correct terms for what they need.
- **AI Streamlines Simple Foraging** — For straightforward cases where users know their goal but lack terminology, generative AI greatly helps by accepting wordy descriptions and natural language without requiring proper keywords.
- **Articulation Barriers Persist** — More complex than simple keyword foraging, users often struggle to explain a problem they don't fully understand themselves; articulation challenges require AI systems to ask clarifying questions and provide context.
- **Discoverability is a Major Barrier** — Even when AI can help, users don't know what questions to ask or how to fully leverage AI's capabilities; the opaque nature of AI makes it difficult for users to discover and understand its full range of uses.
- **Users Aren't Prompt Engineers** — Expecting users to provide extensive context and write detailed prompts burdens them with high interaction cost; instead, AI systems should ask clarifying questions to help users articulate their needs.
- **AI Doesn't Eliminate Information Challenges** — Despite AI's benefits, users still need help naming what they want, hallucinations remain a reliability concern, and the UI design of current AI tools fails to support full discoverability.
