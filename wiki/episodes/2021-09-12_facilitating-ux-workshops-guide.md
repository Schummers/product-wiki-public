# Facilitating UX Workshops: Study Guide

## Summary

Workshops are indispensable UX tools for uncovering diverse perspectives, promoting mutual understanding, and fostering inclusive decision-making. Unlike meetings suited for information sharing, well-designed workshops enable hands-on collaborative problem-solving. This study guide compiles NN/g resources organized by category: structuring and planning workshops, selecting activities and methods, facilitation and participant management, guides for specific workshop types (journey mapping, design studios, service blueprinting, roadmapping), and tips for remote workshops. Effective workshops require careful planning including agenda design, participant selection, facilitation training, and activity selection. Workshops can be adapted to fit team needs and budgets, and remote workshops offer inclusion and convenience while presenting different facilitation challenges.

## Key Takeaways

- **Workshops vs. Meetings** — Meetings share information; workshops enable hands-on problem-solving and collaborative decision-making through structured activities, discussion, and co-creation of artifacts.
- **Systematic Planning** — Effective workshops result from good planning: clear agendas (6-step process), careful participant selection, facilitation preparation, and appropriate activity selection for the workshop goals.
- **Diverge and Converge** — Many workshop activities follow a diverge-then-converge pattern: participants first generate diverse ideas individually, then collaborate to narrow to agreed outcomes, balancing breadth and focus.
- **Participant Management** — Facilitation challenges include encouraging full participation, managing dominating participants, and facilitating with stakeholders present; techniques include intentional silence, parking lots for off-topic items, and explicit guidance.
- **Multiple Workshop Types** — Different structured workshop formats serve specific purposes: journey mapping workshops explore customer experiences, design studios generate concepts, service-blueprinting workshops map service delivery, and roadmapping workshops plan strategy.

## Concepts

- [[UX Workshop]] — A UX workshop is a structured collaborative session designed to uncover perspectives, solve problems, and make inclusive decisions; workshops employ specific activities and facilitation techniques to enable hands-on work rather than information sharing.
- [[Workshop Facilitation]] — Workshop facilitation is the skill of guiding diverse participants through structured activities to achieve workshop goals; it requires preparation, active listening, managing personalities, and ensuring all voices contribute meaningfully.
- [[Team Collaboration]] — Collaborative design involves teams working together on design problems through workshops, ideation, and co-creation; it ensures diverse perspectives inform decisions and builds team alignment around outcomes.
