# Quantifying and Comparing Ease of Use Without Breaking the Bank

## Summary

The PURE method (Pragmatic Usability Rating by Experts) addresses gaps in traditional UX metrics. While behavioral metrics (clicks, conversion rates) and attitudinal metrics (surveys, satisfaction scores) provide limited insights and are either indirect indicators or expensive to collect, PURE offers a practical, cost-effective way to quantify ease of use. Expert panels rate task difficulty on a 1-3 scale, aggregate scores by task and product, and visualize friction levels with colors (green, yellow, red). This method enables quick, frequent, and comparable measurement suitable for competitive analysis and version comparison.

## Key Takeaways

- **PURE measures friction, not experience** — Focuses specifically on ease of use for target users completing fundamental tasks, rated on a 1-3 scale reflecting cognitive load and task difficulty.
- **Aggregated expert judgment replaces expensive studies** — Three usability experts rate steps silently, then discuss and agree on scores, producing reliable, valid results at a fraction of benchmarking costs.
- **Color and numeric combination drives action** — Green (easy), yellow (moderate effort), and red (difficult/failure-prone) make friction visible; a single red step colors the entire product, signaling critical failure points.
- **Focuses on fundamental tasks only** — Identifies 3-20 critical tasks (those essential for business or user core needs) rather than evaluating every possible path.
- **Interrater reliability ensures consistency** — Targets IRR of 0.667+ (typically 0.8-0.9 after training); disagreement signals different assumptions that must be discussed and resolved.
- **Happy path evaluation** — Scores the most desired, optimal flow for target users, allowing teams to focus improvement effort on the best-case scenario before optimizing alternatives.
- **Enables competitive and iterative comparison** — Same fundamental tasks and target audiences across products make PURE scores directly comparable, motivating teams when they see competitive gaps.

## Quotes

> PURE is a usability-evaluation method in which usability experts assign one or more quantitative ratings to a design based on a set of criteria and then combine all these ratings into a final score and easy-to-understand visual representation.

> The numbers and colors shown in PURE scores represent friction, the opposite of ease of use. The higher the number and the "hotter" the colors, the more friction there is — similar to usability-severity ratings.

> When business stakeholders have an easy to understand, numeric representation of an important aspect of their product or service, like ease of use, they tend to be highly motivated to improve on it, and may set goals to do so.

## Concepts

- [[UX Metrics]] — PURE provides an alternative metrics approach filling the gap between expensive benchmarking studies and indirect behavioral metrics, enabling frequent tracking.
- [[Heuristic Evaluation]] — Uses structured expert panel evaluation with silent individual ratings followed by group discussion to produce reliable, validated scores.
- [[Ease of Use Measurement]] — Focuses exclusively on ease of use (not aesthetics, effectiveness, or emotions), measuring friction imposed by task steps on target users.
- [[Benchmarking]] — Enables cost-effective competitive and iterative benchmarking by scoring the same fundamental tasks across product versions or competitors.
