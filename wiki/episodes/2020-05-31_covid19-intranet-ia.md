# How to Organize COVID-19 Information on Your Intranet

## Summary

As organizations respond to urgent information needs during crises like COVID-19, intranet teams face choices about organizing rapidly changing content. Two primary strategies emerge: consolidating all crisis-related information in one dedicated section with homepage links, or integrating it into existing information architecture. Short-term consolidation is fastest to implement and easiest to maintain, making it suitable for urgent situations with limited team capacity. However, organizations must plan for converting temporary information into permanent, discoverable content by determining when temporary items become long-lasting and where they belong permanently. Critical mistakes include leaving information in temporary places indefinitely and failing to update or remove outdated procedures. Successful intranet teams phase implementation, use version-phase communication, and maintain current update dates so users understand information freshness.

## Key Takeaways

- **Two approaches serve different timelines** — consolidating all crisis content in one place serves urgent needs, while integrating into IA works better long-term but requires more upfront work.
- **Consolidation enables rapid updates and clear maintenance** — housing all related content in one location with homepage prominence makes it easy for both users to find information and for content managers to maintain it.
- **Permanent information must not stay in temporary locations** — a common, severe mistake is leaving news items, policies, or tools in temporary sections forever rather than moving them to proper permanent locations.
- **Crosslinking enables content discovery** — when content lives in multiple locations, crosslinking between related pages and improving metadata (keywords, tags, titles) makes search more effective.
- **Phase changes explicitly and mark outdated content** — communicate which phase is current, which are completed, and which are planned; explicitly mark deprecated procedures so users know they're no longer valid.
- **Update dates and version information build trust** — showing when content was last updated helps users understand currency; version numbers and date fields help users distinguish current from outdated procedures.

## Quotes

> "Getting COVID-19 information up on intranets and ensuring that it's accurate and findable is the main focus today. But plan for tomorrow, when things begin to normalize."

> "A policy may start as a news item and then live in the News section of the intranet forever, never updated, moved, or linked to from related pages of the site."

> "Your plan and actions to convert short-term to long-term information will be a longstanding effort, given the scientific predictions that we won't be going back to the old ways of in-person interaction for some time."
