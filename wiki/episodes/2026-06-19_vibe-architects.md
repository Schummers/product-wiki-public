# Vibe Architects: Agentic Vibe Coders

## Summary

Vibe architects are nontechnical people building complex, proactive agentic systems using AI tools without deep technical knowledge of how those systems work. They learn primarily through experimentation and community (Twitter, Reddit, YouTube, Slack), not through product documentation. Most spend 4-8 hours per day with AI tools, continuously reevaluating as models and products change. Despite hundreds of hours of use, opacity persists; even advanced users develop intuitive senses rather than clear understanding. Systems decay over time as connections expire and context drifts. The article argues that while the promise of conversational AI is that anyone can use it intuitively, current products still require years of immersion to achieve competence—functioning as apprenticeships rather than intuitive interfaces.

## Key Takeaways

- **Vibe Architects Build Complex Systems** — Nontechnical people are building sophisticated agentic systems for themselves and their teams; complexity spans data orchestration, multiagent coordination, and workflow automation.
- **Community Over Documentation** — AI products themselves consistently fail as learning sources; vibe architects learn from other practitioners via social media, not from product docs or the AI itself.
- **Massive Time Investment Required** — Advanced users invest 4-8 hours per day; they continuously reevaluate as tools and models change; this filters for people willing to absorb continuous churn.
- **Opacity Persists at Mastery** — Even with thousands of hours, understanding remains hazy; vibe architects develop intuition and instincts rather than clear mental models; they can't articulate how they know what they know.
- **Systems Decay Over Time** — Built-together systems are messy but customized; they decay as connections expire and context drifts; maintenance becomes a recurring tax on the architect's time.

## Quotes

> Generative AI lets people's technical capability outpace their technical knowledge in ways that no previous technology has.

> the **real value of these hacked-together agentic systems is in how customized they are.** Vibe architects know their systems are messy, yet they're uniquely tailored to them and their context, and can be finetuned as their creators' needs change.

> **Going with the vibe is not a shortcut to the "real" way of using these tools -- it is the practice.**
