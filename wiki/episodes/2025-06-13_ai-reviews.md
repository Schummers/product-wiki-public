# AI Summaries of Reviews

## Summary

AI-generated summaries of customer reviews can improve ecommerce experiences by aggregating feedback into digestible overviews, but only when designed carefully. Research with study participants shows that users appreciate AI summaries as a time-saving feature, yet they do not eliminate the desire to read individual reviews. For AI summaries to build trust and truly enhance decision-making, designers must expose negative themes, provide product-specific insights rather than generic statements, and connect summaries to actual reviews through counts, quotes, and links.

Trust remains the critical barrier to adoption. While users recognize AI summarization as a strength, they harbor justified concerns about accuracy and bias. Implementing design practices that surface data sources, include balanced representation, and maintain transparency about AI involvement can significantly improve both trust and utility.

## Key Takeaways

- **AI summaries supplement, not replace** — users appreciate summaries for efficiency but still read individual reviews for specific details and authentic experiences.
- **Summary vagueness undermines value** — generic statements like "great product" apply to any item and fail to guide decision-making; product-specific insights aligned with user concerns are essential.
- **Negative themes build trust** — users expect AI to downplay negatives; including concerns honestly helps users feel they're seeing realistic depictions of actual experiences.
- **Source transparency enables verification** — clickable themes that expand to show mention counts, sentiment breakdowns, supporting quotes, and links to full reviews allow users to verify AI claims.
- **Balance traditional and AI methods** — AI summaries work best alongside traditional summarization approaches like histograms, filters, and keyword searches rather than replacing them.
