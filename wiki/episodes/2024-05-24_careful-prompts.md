# CARE: Structure for Crafting AI Prompts

## Summary

The CARE framework (Context, Ask, Rules, Examples) provides a structured approach to writing effective prompts for generative AI tools. By including situational context, specific requests, constraints, and examples, users dramatically improve the quality and relevance of AI outputs. The article demonstrates the framework through a UX example (writing error messages) and acknowledges that effective prompting requires substantial upfront effort but can accelerate workflows once the technique is mastered.

## Key Takeaways

- **CARE provides a mnemonic for prompt structure** — Context (background), Ask (specific request), Rules (constraints), Examples (what you do/don't want) organize information that helps AI understand the task.
- **Context dramatically improves output quality** — providing user profiles, organization mission, project goals, tone of voice, and relevant background information helps AI tailor responses appropriately.
- **Specificity in the Ask matters** — stating the desired role, output format, number of options, steps to follow, and iterative refinement instructions helps AI approach the problem systematically.
- **Rules and constraints focus the output** — providing guidelines (avoid passive voice, stay under 100 characters, use plain language) and product/brand-specific rules guides AI toward desired results.
- **Examples demonstrate preferred patterns** — providing good and bad examples helps AI understand not just what you need but why and what makes solutions effective.
- **Iteration is necessary and expected** — even with CAREful prompts, designers typically need to refine outputs, ask for additional variations, or combine multiple options to achieve desired results.
