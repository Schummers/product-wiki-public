# The Diverge-and-Converge Technique for UX Workshops

## Summary

The diverge-and-converge collaboration method is a structured two-stage technique that combines individual work with group discussion. In the diverge stage, team members work independently to produce insights, designs, or data without group influence. In the converge stage, the team gathers to discuss findings and synthesize them into collective outputs. This approach reduces groupthink, accommodates diverse working styles, and produces higher-quality results by ensuring all voices are heard before collective decisions are made.

## Key Takeaways

- **Diverge stage enables independent thinking** — team members work alone on research, analysis, design, or ideation, avoiding groupthink and allowing introverts to contribute equally.
- **Converge stage synthesizes perspectives** — after independent work, the team discusses findings and combines insights into unified outputs, identifying patterns and building shared understanding.
- **Timing depends on the activity** — diverging can happen before a workshop (participants work at their own pace) or during it (dedicated solo time in a meeting).
- **Reduce bias through ground rules** — enforcing "quiet time" during diverge, banning "no" or "but," considering anonymity for sensitive topics, and choosing appropriate tools prevents groupthink and dominant voices.
- **Use appropriate tools for each stage** — tools that minimize distraction work best for diverge; synchronous tools that enforce timeboxing work better for converge.
- **Multiple scenarios benefit from this method** — research analysis, mapping workshops, design thinking sessions, and ideation all benefit when individual insights are generated before collective synthesis.

## Quotes

> "Many of the issues that emerge in collaborative workshops happen precisely because team members are not given the time to first sit with their ideas and insights before subjecting them to the opinions of others."

> "Each person's perspective, in isolation, is rarely a complete understanding of the problem."

> "Diverge-and-converge is a powerful tactic that eliminates bias, broadens insight, adds depth to conversations."

- [[Ideation]] — diverge-and-converge is particularly effective for ideation sessions where quantity of ideas is encouraged before evaluation, countering the pressure to filter ideas during initial generation.

- [[Team Collaboration]] — diverge-and-converge improves team collaboration by ensuring all voices are heard and preventing dominant personalities from shaping outcomes prematurely.
- [[Workshop Facilitation]] — the method counteracts groupthink, the HiPPO effect, and personal bias by structuring group work to allow individual perspectives to emerge before collective decision-making.
