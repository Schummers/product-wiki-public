# UX Is Dead, Long Live UX

## Summary

While business leaders question UX value and predict AI will replace designers, this misunderstands where UX value is heading. Micro-level interface optimization has matured; the next frontier is orchestrating complete customer journeys spanning months, channels, and technologies. Product-centric design optimized individual screens down to form fields and button copy, but these micro-optimizations no longer generate significant business value. Journey-centric design addresses the macro experience, applying user-centered principles across touchpoints and channels. Combined with AI, journey-centric design positions organizations to create lasting competitive advantages by leveraging AI's efficiency while maintaining human-centered value propositions.

## Key Takeaways

- **Micro-level interface design no longer generates significant business value** — Product-centric design has optimized individual screens to the point of diminishing returns; business leaders who believe UX is "done" fundamentally misunderstand where value creation is heading.
- **Shift focus from moments to complete journeys** — The paradigm must move from designing individual product interactions to orchestrating customer journeys across months, channels, and touchpoints; journey-centric design parallels Disney's evolution from animated shorts to feature films.
- **Journey-centric design requires organizational operating-system upgrades** — Just as outdated computer operating systems cannot support modern tasks effectively, organizations must upgrade their operational approach to accommodate journey-centric design's cross-functional, long-term perspective.
- **Journey-centric design enables measurement and ROI attribution** — Macro-level journey optimization creates measurable business value and clearer ROI attribution for design investments, making it difficult for leaders to dismiss UX value.
- **AI is instrumental for journey-centric success but requires proper foundations** — Applied to outdated operating systems, AI delivers shallow returns; upgrading organizational structure to journey-centric practices amplifies AI's potential and maximizes profitability.
- **Human-centered design remains essential alongside AI** — The winning recipe combines journey-centric design, human-centered value propositions, and AI-driven solutions for personalization and service delivery optimization.

## Quotes

> The next frontier isn't about perfecting individual screens or workflows — it's about orchestrating complete customer journeys that span months, channels, and technologies.

> There is much more innovation possible and many opportunities for UX problem solving. UX can still bring in business value.

> Like Disney, we should be designing the larger experience, not just the moments.
