# Brand Is Experience in the Digital Age

## Summary

In the digital age, brand is no longer just a logo or slogan but rather the sum of all experiences a person has with a product or company. Brand is defined as "a subjective perception of value based on the sum of a person's experiences with a product or company that ultimately influences that person's sentiment and decisions in the marketplace." A brand is expressed through three equally important components: visuals (graphic elements like logos and typefaces), tone (communication style across all channels), and behavior (how the company acts). The article argues that in digital systems, user experience has become a crucial brand differentiator because customers experience brands through interactive interfaces and expect consistent delivery of brand promise across all touchpoints.

## Key Takeaways

- **Brand is now an experience** — Modern brand definition encompasses the totality of user experiences across all channels rather than being limited to visual representations like logos or slogans.
- **Three components of brand** — Brand is expressed equally through visuals (graphic elements), tone (communication style), and behavior (company actions and values).
- **Digital experience shapes brand perception** — Because all brands now have digital presence, customers have frequent interactive experiences with companies, making behavior a crucial brand attribute.
- **UX is a brand differentiator** — When customers cannot differentiate how they feel about a brand from how they feel about their experiences with it, exceptional UX becomes the key competitive advantage.
- **Consistency across all channels is essential** — Only when visuals, tone, and behaviors are present and true to the core brand message across all interactions do customers have a consistent user experience.

## Quotes

> Definition: Brand is a subjective perception of value based on the sum of a person's experiences with a product or company that ultimately influences that person's sentiment and decisions in the marketplace.

> Brand is not made of visuals or words alone — it's not a logo or a slogan. Nor is it a figurehead, such as Steve Jobs. Those things are simply ways to communicate the brand. Although a figurehead such as Elon Musk or a logo such as McDonald's golden arches can and do serve to effectively deliver the brand message, ultimately, a brand is formulated through a larger set of experiences.

> Most people can't differentiate how they feel about a brand from how they feel about the experiences they have with that brand, so in many situations, UX becomes the brand differentiator.

## Concepts

- [[Branding]] — Modern branding extends beyond visual identity to encompass all customer experiences and consistent delivery across visuals, tone, and behavior; digital UX is a critical component of brand expression, and inconsistency across channels damages brand perception.
- [[User Experience]] — User experience is now inseparable from brand perception; customers judge brands largely by their interactive experiences.
- [[Interaction Design]] — Interaction design details—from transitions to animations to overall system behavior—communicate brand attributes and influence customer perceptions of professionalism and competence.
- [[User Retention]] — Strong, consistent brand experiences drive customer loyalty and differentiation in competitive markets.
