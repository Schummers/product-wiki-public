# Continuous Discovery Habits: Introduction

## Summary

Teresa Torres shares her personal journey from product leader to product discovery coach. She describes her burnout at a venture-backed startup despite loving the work, which led her to shift careers to coaching product teams. Over seven years of coaching, she developed a set of continuous discovery habits that help teams create successful products by obsessing about customer needs. This book aims to share those habits so readers can spend more time with their customers and build better products.

## Key Takeaways

- **Discovery coaching emerged from burnout** — Torres left a successful role not from working long hours but from the exhaustion of constantly advocating for customer-centric product management in a founder-led startup culture.
- **Business lacked better models** — Many companies, especially startups, did not know what "good" product management looked like, leading them to chase sales and competitor obsession rather than customer focus.
- **Coaching curriculum is a product** — Torres applied product thinking to her own coaching, measuring outcomes across cohorts, identifying where teams got stuck, and iterating on her methods.
- **Continuous discovery habits work** — Teams that completed her 12-week coaching program equipped themselves with habits to discover, iterate, and refine products that create customer value and business value.
- **Book's purpose** — The book shares these habits with the hope that readers will be inspired to spend more time with customers and make the world need better products by building them.

## Quotes

> "I realized that, if I worked to change one startup at a time, I'd burn myself out. Instead, I took some time to reflect on how I could have a bigger impact, and I realized that companies fell into the trap of chasing the next sale or obsessing about their competitors because many companies (especially startups) didn't have a better model for product management."

> "Today, teams walk away from my 12-week coaching program equipped with an arsenal of continuous discovery habits that help them discover, iterate, and refine products that deliver value for their customers in a way that drives value for their businesses."

> "My goal with this book is to share those habits with you in the hopes that you, too, will be inspired to spend more time with your customers. The world needs better products. It's up to us to make that happen."

## Concepts

- [[Product Discovery]] — Torres built a systematic approach to product discovery that teams can adopt continuously rather than episodically, based on the core insight that companies fail when they don't obsess about customer needs, pain points, and desires; this approach teaches habits to make customer understanding systematic.
- [[Product Outcome]] — The coaching curriculum teaches teams how to measure and drive outcomes that matter to both customers and businesses.
