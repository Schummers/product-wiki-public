# UX Writing: FAQs from Practitioners

## Summary

This article compiles frequently asked questions from UX writing practitioners, organized by theme: plain language, tone of voice, jargon, structure, and AI integration. The core premise is that plain language benefits all literacy levels, including highly educated experts who are busy and scanning. When content is well-structured and concise, users read a higher proportion of what is essential. The article addresses practical concerns about AI-assisted writing, the shift from traffic-based to conversion-based metrics, and the ongoing relevance of SEO in an AI-mediated search landscape. It emphasizes that good UX writing and good SEO are not in conflict but mutually reinforcing.

## Key Takeaways

- **Aim for 6th-8th Grade Reading Level** — Plain language helps all literacy levels, including experts; users read only 20-28% of page text, so clarity matters more than vocabulary sophistication for ensuring the right content gets noticed.
- **Plain Language Helps Neurodiverse Users** — Chunked content, descriptive subheadings, and bulleted lists benefit users with dyslexia, ADHD, and other cognitive differences; simplification helps all literacy levels.
- **Voice Is Stable, Tone Is Contextual** — Brand voice stays consistent, but tone adapts to the situation (empathetic in error messages, encouraging in onboarding); style guides should document both stable voice and tone-shift guidance.
- **AI Can Generate First Drafts** — AI is useful for headlines, alternatives, alt text, and brainstorming, but it drifts from brand voice and lacks knowledge of users and context; always edit with audience in mind.
- **Search Still Matters** — SEO, AEO, and GEO share the same underlying principles: clear structure, plain language, direct answers, and genuine authority; websites shift from destinations to sources that AI tools cite.

## Concepts

- [[Plain Language]] — Clear, concise writing at a 6th-8th grade level improves comprehension for all audiences and all literacy levels; it also benefits users with cognitive differences and reduces scan time.
- [[Content Strategy]] — Effective content strategy balances AI-assisted creation with strategic oversight; it requires understanding users, testing copy with real people, and maintaining focus on task completion over traffic metrics.
- [[Tone and Voice]] — Tone is contextual and adaptive to emotional state; voice is stable and reflects organizational personality; both must be considered in style guides and testing with actual users.
