# Site Search Suggestions

## Summary

Search suggestions (autocomplete or autosuggest) have become an expected feature of well-designed site search. They help users by allowing them to select from recommended queries rather than typing complete queries themselves. While users expect this feature and sometimes comment when it is absent, they don't always actually use suggestions even when offered. When implemented effectively, search suggestions can provide several benefits including reducing interaction cost, helping users avoid typos, and informing users about the range of products or options available on a site.

## Key Takeaways

- **Avoid irrelevant results** — Every suggested query must produce good and relevant results. Suggesting queries that return zero or irrelevant results is worse than providing no suggestions at all.
- **Use text styling to differentiate** — Different visual styles should distinguish between user-typed characters and system-suggested characters to help users understand why suggestions appear and scan available options.
- **Include scope in suggestions** — For scoped sites, visually distinguish the scope of suggestions to clarify which content area will be searched (e.g., category name, department).
- **Consider rich suggestions** — Beyond simple query recommendations, the dropdown can include links to category pages, product pages, and articles, though this works best for sites with visually diverse products.
- **Apply mobile simplification** — On smaller devices, simplify rich search suggestions to display only text suggestions rather than complex multi-content dropdowns.

## Quotes

> Search suggestions are recommended queries that appear in a dropdown as users type in a search box. These recommendations appear beneath the search box and change as users type each letter of their query.

> In our recent study on ecommerce websites, suggested queries were selected by users in only 23% of the instances where they were offered.

> For site search, search suggestions have the further benefit of informing users of the range of products or options offered by the site. Conversely, if something isn't shown as a search suggestion after entering the first few characters of a product name, some users may conclude that the site doesn't offer that product and abandon the site without even executing the search.

## Concepts

- [[Search]] — Search suggestions have become expected on well-designed e-commerce sites; effective implementation requires guidance on differentiating typed and suggested text, handling scoped searches, and choosing between rich suggestions and simple text, though user behavior shows suggestions are selected in only 23% of instances where offered.
- [[Interaction Cost]] — Search suggestions reduce interaction cost by letting users select from suggestions rather than typing complete queries, and by helping them avoid typos and mental effort in formulating searches.
