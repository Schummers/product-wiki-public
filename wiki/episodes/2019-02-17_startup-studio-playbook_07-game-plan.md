# Startup Studio Playbook: Game Plan

## Summary

This chapter documents eFounders' approach to building and scaling startups through a structured platform and repeatable processes. Part II of the book presents case studies of venture studios; this chapter reveals eFounders' "Game Plan"—an exhaustive 18-month operational framework that organizes the studio's work into phases (inception, build, scale, growth) and structures all activities through a dedicated multidisciplinary team. The authors argue that a studio's primary value lies in its platform and people, and that reproducible processes, combined with deep domain expertise and access to talent networks, enable faster company creation than traditional startup models.

eFounders has built over 10 companies with combined valuations reaching $125M+ and monthly recurring revenue in the millions. Their approach systematizes ideation (20 ideas per year), validates concepts rigorously (keeping 4 per year), recruits exceptional co-founders (100+ applications monthly), and shepherds each venture through structured monthly processes across strategy, product, marketing, and sales—all with the goal of spinning out independent companies after 18 months.

## Key Takeaways

- **Platform is the moat** — the studio's competitive advantage is its documented processes, internal tools (ContactX, knowledge base called "The Books"), multidisciplinary team (11+ specialists), and network of 6,000+ contacts; each of these assets supports multiple ventures simultaneously, reducing per-company startup costs.

- **Ideation at scale requires structure** — monthly "Ideation Breakfasts" where team members pitch ideas, coupled with tools (Feedly for trend tracking, Slack rooms for discussion), generate approximately 20 ideas yearly; a two-month validation process (market analysis, technical analysis, prototype testing) filters these down to 4 projects per year.

- **Co-founder selection is the highest-leverage hire** — eFounders receives 100+ applications monthly for 4 yearly slots; the entry criteria are exceptionally high (entrepreneurs capable of scaling to billion-dollar companies), and every applicant meets the entire team individually before an offer; technical founders must be full-stack builders and future CTOs; business founders must combine analytics, product sense, and leadership.

- **Processes scale without losing flexibility** — the Game Plan contains 166 defined processes spread across 18 months (months 1–9 for Build, months 9–18 for Scale), organized by function (strategy, tech, creative, product, marketing, sales), but the team stays flexible and respects each company's uniqueness; processes are the mechanism for speed and consistency.

- **Routine operations keep teams aligned and focused** — weekly Kick-Off Meetings (30 min, Monday), weekly Product Committees (1 hour, Friday), monthly Sunday emails from CEOs, and quarterly Demo Days with public goal-setting create momentum and accountability; they cost time upfront but prevent misalignment.

- **Network and talent mix are the platform's mortar** — multidisciplinary in-house teams (creative, tech, product, marketing, sales, admin) execute 166 processes without external help; a proprietary contact database enables pilot recruitment, expert consultation, and investor introductions; communities (SaaS Club, Build Together) extend reach and provide beta-test pools.

## Quotes

> Over the years, a studio that builds companies repeatedly like eFounders accumulates know-how, multi-disciplinary expertise and skills. To leverage this considerable knowledge, we've set up processes and an overall structure at the disposal of our projects. We believe it's the only way to create reproducible experiences for each of our creation. That's the reason why processes are at the very heart of our approach.

> eFounders' philosophy is that our startups become 100% operationally independent after 18 months. For instance, it's exactly what's happening to Aircall these days. At that moment, our projects enter the Growth phase. If the studio has done its job well, our habits of processes and our culture is transferred to the project's teams.

> eFounders' processes are built to enable a reproducible experience each time we create a new company. However, the team changes and it's very important for us that all team members frequently put down in writing their knowledge and tips for the next generation.

## Concepts

- [[Venture Studio]] — eFounders operates as a multi-company platform where a core team of 11+ specialists serves as the studio backbone; the key competitive advantage is the ability to execute 166 repeatable processes simultaneously across multiple ventures in parallel, using internal resources and a 6,000-person network.

- [[Studio Platform]] — the studio's value derives from shared infrastructure (processes, tools, team) rather than individual venture success; concrete examples include ContactX (contact management), The Books (knowledge base), Trello (task management), and portfolio access to shared resources (design, tech, marketing, sales, admin).

- [[Product-Market Fit]] — eFounders' Build phase (months 1–9) focuses on confronting an MVP with 25 pilot users daily and confirming product-market fit before scaling; the validation process includes market analysis, technical analysis, prototype testing, and user feedback from the network; Market fit is required before entering the 9–18 month Scale phase.

- [[Founder Recruitment]] — the studio receives 100+ applications per month for 4 annual slots; founders must be entrepreneurs capable of managing billion-dollar companies; technical founders are full-stack developers with future CTO-level leadership potential; business founders combine analytical rigor, product thinking, and stakeholder conviction; all candidates meet the entire team before hiring.

- [[Studio Operations]] — day-to-day work is structured through routine rituals: Sunday CEO emails, Monday Kick-Off Meetings, Friday Product/Marketing Committees, quarterly Demo Days with public goal-setting, and internal knowledge capture (The Books, process sheets); these rituals cost time but prevent misalignment and enable rapid course correction.
