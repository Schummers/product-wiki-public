# Day-0 Calls: Avoid UX-Workshop Disasters by Aligning on the Basics in Advance

## Summary

Day-0 calls are pre-workshop virtual meetings held days to a week before a workshop to align stakeholders on foundational basics like workshop goals, scope, and outcomes. They solve a common problem where workshop disasters occur because key stakeholders arrive with conflicting assumptions about what the workshop is supposed to accomplish. A facilitator revised their entire week-long workshop on the first morning when the CIO revealed the workshop scope was completely misaligned with organizational roadmap capacity. Day-0 calls prevent these expensive surprises by establishing consensus on critical assumptions early, when there is still time to pivot plans. The call should last one hour or less, include all workshop participants plus invested executives, happen a few days to a week before the workshop, and cover the agenda, establish basics, and set participant expectations. This secondary benefit of setting clear expectations reduces day-1 surprises and frustration.

## Key Takeaways

- **Prevents disasters through early alignment** — Day-0 calls move agreement on foundational basics (goals, scope) before the workshop, preventing major pivots on day one when the full group discovers they were working toward different objectives.
- **Essential components are straightforward** — Three components determine success: the agenda (identify assumptions needing buy-in), who attends (entire workshop group plus critical executives), and timing (few days to a week before to allow plan revision if needed).
- **Include all participants, never exclude** — Ninety percent of the time the attendee list is the complete workshop participant list; selectively excluding people thought to be less critical creates risk of later veto or rejection.
- **Agenda focuses on critical assumptions** — Limit discussion to things that could truly derail the workshop; typical agendas focus primarily on workshop goals and setting the stage, leaving other thorny questions for the workshop itself.
- **Obtain explicit buy-in from decider** — Beyond group consensus, get verbal agreement specifically from the person with veto power; their buy-in ensures the workshop can proceed with confidence.
- **Sets participant expectations** — Secondary benefit includes giving participants insight into workshop format and ground rules (e.g., no laptops, full participation); hearing these expectations early gives time for adjustment rather than causing day-1 frustration.

## Concepts

- [[Workshop Facilitation]] — Day-0 calls are a preparation step that prevents common facilitation disasters by providing early information and addressing foundational assumptions, enabling skilled facilitators to adapt on the fly when unexpected information arises and to facilitate effectively without constant pivoting.
- [[Stakeholder Engagement]] — Day-0 calls create explicit alignment on workshop goals and scope before expensive workshop time is spent, obtaining consensus (decisions everyone can live with) that prevents mid-workshop conflicts and major plan revisions.
- [[Pre-workshop Planning]] — Careful planning before a workshop, including participant selection and goal clarification, determines whether the workshop succeeds; day-0 calls are essential preparation.
