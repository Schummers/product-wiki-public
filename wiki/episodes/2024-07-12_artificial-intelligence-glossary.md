# Artificial Intelligence: Glossary

## Summary

This comprehensive glossary defines key terminology used in artificial intelligence and generative AI discussions. It covers foundational concepts like algorithms, machine learning, and neural networks, as well as specialized terms related to large language models, training methods, and AI capabilities. The glossary serves as a reference guide for UX professionals and others learning about AI technologies.

## Key Takeaways

- **AI basics are foundational** — understanding core concepts like algorithms, datasets, and models is essential for discussing any AI application or limitation.
- **LLMs use probabilistic prediction** — large language models generate text by predicting the most likely next word based on training data, not by accessing true knowledge.
- **Training shapes AI behavior** — how models are trained, what data they learn from, and the parameters set during training all influence what the model can do.
- **Prompting techniques matter** — different prompting strategies like chain-of-thought, zero-shot, and few-shot prompting change how effectively AI systems respond.
- **AI has inherent limitations** — hallucinations, hallucination, overfitting, and other failure modes are built into probabilistic models and remain active research areas.
