# Accelerating Research with AI

## Summary

Kate Moran and Maria Rosala provide comprehensive guidance on where AI tools can effectively accelerate UX research and where they have significant limitations. The article maps AI capabilities across the research lifecycle: planning (desk research, ideation, documentation), conducting research (notetaking, interviews), analyzing data (transcription, preliminary coding, quantitative analysis), and reporting. Key findings emphasize that AI excels at text-based work and administrative tasks but cannot truly observe behavior or watch usability testing, and that human oversight and expert review remain critical throughout the research process.

## Key Takeaways

- **AI accelerates planning and documentation** — AI helps generate research questions, creates study templates, and drafts consent forms and recruitment materials, reducing tedious administrative work.
- **AI cannot observe user behavior** — AI tools cannot truly watch usability tests or observe nonverbal interactions; they can only analyze transcripts or look at what users say, not what they do.
- **AI excels at text analysis** — transcription, summarization, preliminary coding, and initial clustering of qualitative data can be accelerated by AI, but human experts must review and refine the results.
- **AI works best with human oversight** — treating AI like an intern that needs correction and guidance produces better research outcomes than expecting it to work autonomously.
- **Human judgment remains essential** — contextual understanding, cultural awareness, and the ability to connect dots across data are capabilities only human researchers possess; AI analysis should be a starting point only.

## Quotes

> Current AI tools have many limitations.[Like interns,](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-intern/) they work best when you provide ample instructions, context, constraints, and corrections.

> Never rely on AI tools to perform all your analysis for you. AI is stochastic — it can choose to pay attention to certain things but disregard others. That might mean it'll focus on the wrong aspects of your data.

> Use AI transcription, summarization, and coding features to **speed up the initial steps in your analysis process.**
