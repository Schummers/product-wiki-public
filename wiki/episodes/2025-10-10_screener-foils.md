# Stop Misrecruits: Add Foils to Your Screener

## Summary

Even carefully designed screeners can let misrecruits slip through—people who don't match the target audience, discovered halfway through a study. Foils are deliberately false answer options or questions designed to quickly reveal who doesn't belong. A nonexistent cloud storage service name in a list reveals respondents clicking through without attention. Foil answer options work better than full foil questions because they blend seamlessly into real options. When designed with three principles—plausible, relevant, and used sparingly—foils protect data quality by catching attentive participants early without confusing genuine respondents.

## Key Takeaways

- **Foils reveal inattentive and dishonest participants** — A foil is a deliberately false answer that catches respondents clicking through, guessing to qualify, or intentionally misrepresenting themselves; selecting it flags data reliability concerns.
- **Foil answer options work better than foil questions** — Integrated into real answer options, foil answers process quickly without burdening qualified participants; full foil questions add unnecessary length and complexity.
- **Make foils plausible but not absurd** — A foil must blend into the real options ("Previewing your driver's music playlist") not stand out as nonsensical ("Controlling the car like a remote control"); plausibility is key to catching inattentive respondents.
- **Keep foils relevant to the topic** — A foil for streaming services should sound like a service brand, not random; relevance ensures it tests actual attention, not just nonsense detection.
- **Use foils sparingly—one or two per screener** — Multiple foils confuse even genuine participants and create excessive complexity; focused use of one foil per question is sufficient to catch misrecruits.
- **Combine foils with other safeguards** — Foils catch some misrecruits but not all; pair with open-ended questions requiring specific knowledge, consistency checks across questions, and validation during live sessions for strongest results.
