# In-Store & Online: Designing for the Changing Behaviors of Today's Shoppers

## Summary

This article examines how customer shopping behaviors have evolved to blend online and offline experiences. Rather than choosing between online or in-store shopping, customers now seamlessly move between channels throughout their journey. Showrooming (visiting stores to examine products before buying online) and webrooming (researching online before purchasing in-store) are now common behaviors. The article argues that retailers should acknowledge these mixed-channel behaviors and design their customer journeys to either eliminate unnecessary transitions or make transitions easy when they are unavoidable.

## Key Takeaways

- **Customers blur the line between online and in-store shopping** — People view shopping as a unified experience and move between channels based on convenience and specific needs, not as discrete either/or choices.

- **Showrooming and webrooming are established behaviors** — Customers browse physical stores to touch and inspect products before making final purchases online for cost savings, while others research online to make in-store visits more efficient.

- **Channel transitions are opportunities for lost sales** — Each transition between online and offline represents a risk that customers will abandon their purchase; minimizing unnecessary transitions increases conversion likelihood.

- **Blend benefits by providing comprehensive product information online** — High-quality photography, detailed descriptions, robust reviews, and close-up images reduce the need to visit stores, allowing customers to feel confident purchasing online.

- **Facilitate transitions with clear directions and preparation** — When customers must move between channels, use confirmation emails with clear directions, parking information, and instructions on what to have ready to support the transition.

- **Physical stores remain valuable as discovery and testing spaces** — Real-world locations serve a critical role allowing customers to try, touch, and feel products, but should be integrated with digital options for comparison and additional selection.

## Concepts

- [[Omnichannel Experience]] — the integration of online and offline channels to create a seamless customer experience across touchpoints and reducing friction in channel transitions.

- [[E-Commerce]] — how online shopping has evolved from a separate channel to one component of a larger shopping ecosystem requiring integration with physical retail.

- [[Customer Journey]] — mapping the paths customers take across channels and identifying where transitions occur and where friction can be reduced.

- [[Showrooming]] — the behavior of visiting physical stores to inspect and evaluate products before making purchases online, treating retail locations as discovery spaces.

- [[Product Information]] — how comprehensive online product details, photography, and specifications can reduce the need for in-store visits by allowing confident purchasing decisions.
