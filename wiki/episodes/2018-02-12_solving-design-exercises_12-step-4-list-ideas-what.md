# Solving Product Design Exercises: Step 4: List ideas (What)

## Summary

This chapter explains Step 4 of the framework, where designers generate possible product solutions to fulfill customer needs identified in Step 3. Dashinsky demonstrates how varying product properties (type, platform, interface) reveals a spectrum of solution possibilities beyond the obvious first impression. He provides the Waze bike navigation example to show how exploring different platforms (physical device, mobile app, smartwatch) and interface types (visual, haptic, voice) leads to more innovative solutions.

## Key Takeaways

- **Property Exploration** — Generate multiple ideas by varying product properties: type (digital vs. physical), platform (smartwatch, smartphone, desktop, VR, etc.), and interface type (graphic, audio/voice, VR, AR), expanding beyond the initial obvious solution.
- **Multiple Solution Paths** — The same user need can be solved through different product forms; for bike navigation, solutions range from a physical steering wheel device with LEDs to a smartwatch app with haptic feedback to a smartphone app with voice guidance.
- **Combining Platform and Interface** — Different platforms enable different interface possibilities (e.g., smartwatch enables haptic feedback, smartphone enables voice-in-pocket navigation); considering these combinations yields richer solution options.
- **Template for Ideation** — Use the structure "Build X for [Who], that [When and Where] to [Why]" to generate solution ideas systematically by combining audience, context, and motivation from previous steps.
- **Three or four, then stop** — The step asks for three or four possible products, listed without weighing them; the evaluation criteria and the impact/effort matrix belong to Step 5. The chapter sets no rule about suspending judgment, it simply places the judgment in the next step.

## Quotes

> Your first impression of how Waze could serve bike riders might be adding another mode to their mobile app. But exploring different properties can help you to think about a wider spectrum of possible products that could achieve that — digital or physical, mobile app or smartwatch app, graphic interface or voice interface and more.

> If you feel a little bit stuck, you can use this template to kick off the process:
>
> Build X for \<Who / Step 2\>, that \<When and Where / Step 3\> to \<Why / Step 1\>.
