# User-Experience Quiz: 2020 UX Year in Review

## Summary

A 10-question multiple-choice quiz designed to assess UX practitioners' knowledge across core UX concepts and research findings from Nielsen Norman Group's body of work. The quiz covers topics including accelerators versus commands, the effects of animated GIFs on email perception, mobile app walkthroughs, dark mode usability, information scent, research methods, accordion signifiers, and contextual inquiry. The quiz serves as both an educational tool and a self-assessment mechanism for UX professionals to gauge their expertise level.

## Key Takeaways

- **Animated GIFs in emails are perceived negatively** — research shows users perceive emails with animated GIFs less positively compared to static emails, contrary to intuitive expectations.
- **Walkthroughs do not improve task performance** — users who complete mobile app walkthroughs actually perform worse on subsequent tasks and are less likely to successfully complete them.
- **Dark mode has specific usability tradeoffs** — dark mode decreases visual acuity in people with normal vision, contrary to assumptions about universal visual fatigue reduction.
- **Information scent is user-dependent** — information scent varies from user to user and depends on the link label and the user's goal, not just objective page content.
- **Critical-incident technique is a specific research method** — it is a qualitative method in which participants recall events with significant outcomes, distinct from expert review or analytics methods.
