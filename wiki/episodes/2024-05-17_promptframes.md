# Promptframes: Evolving the Wireframe for the Age of AI

## Summary

Promptframes are a new design deliverable that document content goals and requirements for generative AI prompts, positioned between traditional wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes. They bridge the gap between layout planning and realistic content by using AI to rapidly generate contextually appropriate placeholder content for testing and feedback. This approach improves content fidelity earlier in the design process, enabling better user testing feedback and faster iteration cycles.

## Key Takeaways

- **Promptframes document AI prompt objectives and constraints** — they sit between wireframes and prototypes, specifying what content should be generated, why, and under what constraints.
- **They solve placeholder-content problems** — poor placeholder content (like lorem ipsum) prevents meaningful user feedback; promptframes generate realistic content faster so users can evaluate actual information.
- **Multiple prompts address different content types** — copy, images, data visualizations, and other content types each require specific prompt parameters (tone, style, dimensions, constraints) documented in the promptframe.
- **Context must be shared with the AI tool** — providing user personas, brand voice, organization mission, and visual principles to the AI increases output quality and relevance.
- **Iteration is built into the workflow** — promptframes support rapid refinement; feedback from testing can be incorporated by revising prompts and regenerating content quickly.
- **Final content still requires human craft** — promptframes accelerate feedback cycles but do not replace the human effort to create production-ready, polished content.

## Concepts

- [[AI Prompts]] — promptframes systematize prompt writing by documenting objectives, constraints, tone, and examples that guide AI generation of contextually appropriate content.
- [[Generative AI]] — Generative AI serves as a tool within the UX design workflow that accelerates iteration by providing rapid, contextually relevant content variations (copy, images, data) for testing, replacing generic placeholders in prototypes.
- [[Wireframing]] — promptframes extend traditional wireframing by adding a new stage that bridges wireframes and prototypes, focusing on content fidelity before detailed design.
- [[Documentation]] — promptframes represent a new category of deliverable that designers can use to collaborate with AI tools and document their content strategy.
- [[Prototyping]] — promptframes enable faster prototyping with more realistic content, improving the quality of feedback from colleagues and users in testing sessions.
