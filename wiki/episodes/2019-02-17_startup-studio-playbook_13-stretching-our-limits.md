# Startup Studio Playbook: Stretching our limits

## Summary

This chapter chronicles Drukka's transformation from a software development and online marketing agency into a factory capable of launching dozens of viable startups annually. Beginning with EUR 100,000 seed capital and an ambitious mantra of "build startups by the dozen," the studio systematized lean validation (10,000-20,000 USD per initiative) and tested copycat sharing-economy models to reduce market risk. By 2017, Drukka had evolved into a formally structured venture builder with external co-investors, a dedicated pre-seed fund (Studioone.hu), and partnerships with universities and corporations.

The chapter emphasizes scaling challenges: managing resource conflicts among parallel ventures, fundraising trust-building with skeptical investors, and cost discipline required to produce dozens of startups from limited capital. It shows how studios scale beyond 2-3 ventures annually through batching by industry, outsourcing CEO pre-validation, and standardizing processes to reach operational efficiency.

## Key Takeaways

- **Cost-Efficient Validation** — With EUR 100,000 initial capital, Drukka allocated 10,000-20,000 USD per startup initiative; this extreme frugality required starting with low-risk copycat ideas in sharing economy space before graduating to proprietary concepts.

- **Fundraising Trust-Building** — Early investors feared cap table dilution and CEO motivation problems; Drukka overcame skepticism by demonstrating traction through spin-offs achieving 13x valuation increases at seed stage and 6-8x during Series A negotiations, vindicating the studio model.

- **Scaling Through Batching** — To move from 6-7 concurrent ventures to a dozen annually, Drukka batched startups by industry, running joint mentoring sessions for related CEOs, discovering code base reuse opportunities, and sharing learning across portfolio companies.

- **Pre-Seed Fund Model** — Studioone.hu formalized the studio-plus-external-capital model: EUR 20-40,000 per startup with Drukka providing infrastructure; from EUR 1 million raised, the fund could build 30-40 startups with the fund holding 65-75% equity, offering co-investors deal flow and portfolio exposure.

- **Portfolio Companies Path to Independence** — Within 18-24 months, seed-funded companies (Rendi.hu, Neatly.hu, Tickething.com, EzExpress.hu) achieved seed funding rounds; the studio remained active in portfolio companies post-spin-off, creating long-term partnerships.

- **CEO Validation Process** — Instead of building multiple product prototypes per CEO, the studio had each incoming CEO develop 3-5 bare-minimum concept validations (offering only, no product) in parallel with investor market testing; this filtered the viable 1-2 ideas from the initial 3-5 candidates.

## Quotes

> We wanted to be the studio that fills up the "startup funnel". And that meant learning how to manage a lot of small bets at the same time.

> Burning through most of the initial capital, Drukka managed to launch a dozen initiatives, getting almost half of them to a seed-ready stage, where the startups already have a working product, growing user base and revenue, and a sustainable growth engine.

> In 2017, after long preparations, we launched a pre-seed investment fund called Studioone.hu, co-financed by Drukka and outside investors. The base idea is that this new fund will invest 20-40.000 USD into new ideas, and Drukka will provide all other resources.

## Concepts

- [[Venture Studio]] — Drukka exemplifies the journey from agency to studio model, demonstrating that the transformation requires deliberate strategy (mantra: "build startups by the dozen"), structured validation processes, and willingness to experiment with parallel initiatives despite resource constraints.

- [[Studio Economics]] — The chapter details capital allocation: EUR 100,000 initial capital, EUR 10-20,000 per initiative, and later Studioone.hu's EUR 20-40,000 per startup fund; profitability came from seed-funded portfolio companies achieving 13x valuation increase at seed and 6-8x at Series A.

- [[Studio Funnel]] — Drukka operationalized a funnel with minimum market research, rapid MVP building, aggressive traction-seeking, business plan and investor deck preparation, and seed fundraising; half of initial dozen initiatives reached seed-ready status within 18-24 months.

- [[Portfolio Strategy]] — By batching related startups (sharing economy), running joint CEO mentoring sessions, and reusing code bases, Drukka discovered portfolio synergies; this batching enabled scaling from 6-7 concurrent ventures to a dozen annually.

- [[Founder Recruitment]] — Drukka recruited young, talented entrepreneurs who had already tried startup or lifestyle business building; these co-founder CEOs valued the studio model because it let them focus on business building without managing basic infrastructure.
