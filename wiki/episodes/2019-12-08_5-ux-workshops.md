# 5 UX Workshops and When to Use Them: A Cheat Sheet

## Summary

UX workshops are intensive collaborative sessions designed to solve specific problems and enable progress on design challenges. Distinct from traditional meetings, workshops involve longer timeframes, deep focus on single issues, and active hands-on participation. Five common workshop types serve different purposes: discovery workshops create consensus on project plans; empathy workshops help teams understand user needs; design workshops rapidly generate multiple solution ideas; prioritization workshops build consensus on which features matter most; critique workshops ensure designs align with user needs. Workshops can be scaled, combined, and adapted based on specific project needs and stakeholder availability.

## Key Takeaways

- **Workshop vs. meeting distinction** — meetings share information passively; workshops solve problems actively through hands-on activities; workshops require deeper time investment and preparation but provide greater buy-in and shared ownership.
- **Discovery workshops** — communicate current state and build consensus on milestones; help teams understand business requirements, gather existing knowledge, and create mutual understanding of direction and vision.
- **Empathy workshops** — help teams prioritize user needs before designing; shift mindset from features-first to users-first; share compelling research insights to prime user-centric thinking.
- **Design workshops** — rapidly generate and discuss multiple solution ideas through sketching and hands-on activities; create shared ownership by inviting crossdisciplinary participation in ideation.
- **Prioritization workshops** — build consensus on feature value and prioritization; address scope creep by refocusing team on what matters most; enable structured voting and ranking activities.
- **Critique workshops** — evaluate designs against user needs and established principles; identify quick fixes and long-term optimizations; use objective criteria to guide discussion.

## Quotes

> UX workshops: Intensive collaborative sessions used to solve problems and enable progress on a particular challenge throughout the design timeline.

> Workshops enable participants to come together for a concentrated time of idea generation and hands-on activities that allow them to achieve an actionable goal.

> Though each workshop is presented individually within this article for the sake of clarity, these workshops can be scaled, combined or otherwise adapted to be more effective or appropriate for specific UX challenges.
