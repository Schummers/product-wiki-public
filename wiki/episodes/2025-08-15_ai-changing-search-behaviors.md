# How AI Is Changing Search Behaviors

## Summary

Generative AI is reshaping how people search for information, though traditional search is far from obsolete. A qualitative study with nine participants explored how AI-powered tools and chatbots are shifting information-seeking habits. Key findings include that search habits are sticky and deeply ingrained, but AI's value in overcoming tedious research tasks is compelling enough to change those habits for some users. AI overviews on search results pages steal attention and often satisfy information needs without clicks, while AI chat tools like Gemini and ChatGPT impress first-time users and lead them to plan further AI use. However, AI doesn't eliminate the need for traditional search, and familiarity with ChatGPT and Gemini provides them competitive advantages in an emerging market.

## Key Takeaways

- **Information-Seeking Habits are Deeply Ingrained** — Most participants default to Google out of long-standing habit and comfort; changing these habits requires significant incentive, which AI provides through substantial shortcuts around tedious research tasks.
- **AI Offers Powerful Value for Information-Seeking** — AI helps overcome keyword-foraging problems, information gaps, source evaluation, information sifting, synthesis, and storage through powerful shortcuts that make research faster and easier.
- **AI Overviews Reduce Click-Through Rates** — Featured at the top of search results, AI overviews answer questions directly and satisfy information needs without requiring users to visit actual content pages, reducing traffic for content sites.
- **AI Chat Impresses First-Time Users** — Participants experienced with AI chat for information-seeking, especially those using it for the first time during the study, shifted their habits immediately and planned to use AI chat for future research tasks.
- **Traditional Search Persists** — All study participants continued using traditional search multiple times, suggesting AI hasn't replaced search but is being used in tandem to fact-check and explore complementary angles.
- **Familiarity is a Competitive Advantage** — ChatGPT captured early adoption and linguistically became referred to simply as "Chat"; Gemini's integration with Google search gives it potential to catch up in the habit-driven market.
