# Data Is More than Numbers: Why Qualitative Data Isn't Just Opinions

## Summary

Qualitative research with small sample sizes is rigorous data-driven inquiry, not anecdotal opinion gathering. While qualitative methods differ fundamentally from quantitative ones, they follow systematic processes grounded in theory, employ established rigor criteria (credibility, transferability, dependability, confirmability), and produce insights unavailable through measurement alone. Small sample sizes are appropriate because qualitative research aims to understand *why* problems occur and *how* to fix them, not to quantify prevalence. Qualitative research complements quantitative measurement by illuminating the human experience—expectations, mental models, pain points, needs—that metrics alone cannot capture.

## Key Takeaways

- **Different Goals, Different Methods** — Qualitative research explores *that* a problem exists and *why*, while quantitative measures how *much* of the population experiences it; both are necessary, but conflating their purposes creates false criticism.
- **Rigor is Systematic** — Qualitative rigor emerges from systematic processes: evidence-based theoretical frameworks, specific research questions, careful participant sampling, open-ended facilitation, systematic coding using inductive reasoning, and triangulation.
- **Small Samples Are Defensible** — If one user encounters a known usability principle violation, that is credible data indicating a real problem, even without quantification; large samples are unnecessary when the goal is to identify and fix problems early.
- **Theoretical Grounding** — Qualitative researchers apply established frameworks (Nielsen's heuristics, cognitive psychology, HCI principles) to interpret observations; a finding that exemplifies a known principle is trustworthy regardless of sample size.
- **Empathy and Humanity** — Qualitative research enables genuine empathetic understanding of users as humans, not just data points; this understanding is critical to ethical design and building teams with diverse lived experiences.

## Quotes

> Not everything that can be counted counts, and not everything that counts can be counted.

> If the first tester accidentally burns themselves on the fry basket because the handle is right over a heating element, I will probably not keep testing it with a large sample to figure out exactly what proportion of users will also burn themselves and sue me.

> Qualitative research is rigorous and systematic, but it has different goals than quantitative measurement.

## Concepts

- [[Qualitative Research]] — Qualitative research systematically gathers insights about user experience, expectations, mental models, and behavior through observation and conversation; it complements quantitative measurement by explaining why problems occur and how to solve them.
- [[Research Validity]] — Qualitative rigor is established through credibility (accurate observation), transferability (applicable in other contexts), dependability (consistent findings), and confirmability (reduced bias); rigor emerges from systematic methodology, not sample size.
- [[Empathy]] — Genuine empathy for users develops through engaging with real people, observing their frustrations, and understanding how they experience the world differently; this empathy is foundational to ethical design and user-centered decision-making.
