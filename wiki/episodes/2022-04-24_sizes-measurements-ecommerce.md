# Size Guides and Product Measurements for International Shoppers

## Summary

International shoppers frequently express concern about whether apparel will fit them, particularly when returns are costly or impossible. This article addresses how to design effective size guides for global ecommerce sites. It distinguishes between localized sites (which should display local sizes prominently) and international sites serving global markets (which need comprehensive conversion charts). The article identifies when single size charts are insufficient and recommends brand-specific guides, body-dimension information, and precise product measurements. Special attention is paid to mobile users who struggle with large conversion tables. Clear documentation of measurement methods helps users choose confidently.

## Key Takeaways

- **Localized sites should list local sizes prominently** alongside original sizes so users don't have to look up conversions.
- **Comprehensive size charts with multiple regional options** serve international sites, including optional filters for different countries.
- **Brand-specific size guides are necessary** because brands vary significantly in sizing, and a single chart cannot account for this variation.
- **Body-dimension information** (bust, waist, hip measurements) helps users match sizes to their actual body, not abstract labels.
- **Product measurements for all sizes** provide precision for users wanting specific fits, but must explain how measurements were taken.
- **Mobile users need adjacent columns** showing conversion between original size and their local size to avoid excessive scrolling.
