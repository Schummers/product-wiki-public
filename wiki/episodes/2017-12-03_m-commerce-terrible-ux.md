# M-Commerce: Terrible UX

## Summary

This article analyzes trends in mobile ecommerce (m-commerce) conversion rates from 2014 to 2017, revealing that mobile users consistently generate lower revenue per visit than desktop users. In 2017, each desktop visitor was worth 111% more than a mobile visitor, a significant improvement from 2014 when the difference was 288%, but still indicating that mobile user experience remains substantially worse than desktop. The article attributes poor mobile conversions not to fundamental user demographics but to inadequate mobile design. Many sites either lack a dedicated mobile design entirely or fail to implement mobile-specific usability guidelines, worsening problems already inherent to the mobile platform (small screens, text input difficulty). The data demonstrates that improving mobile UX could triple revenue from mobile traffic, making it a critical investment priority.

## Key Takeaways

- **Mobile Revenue Gap** — Desktop visitors generate 111% more revenue per visit than mobile visitors in 2017; this gap has narrowed from 288% in 2014, but mobile still substantially underperforms despite representing 40% of traffic.
- **Not a User Problem** — The revenue gap is not caused by differences in user wealth or purchasing power; instead, it reflects poor mobile user experience design that fails to accommodate mobile platform constraints.
- **Platform-Specific Challenges** — Mobile is a weaker UI platform than desktop in terms of screen size and text input; these platform weaknesses make it more critical to follow mobile usability guidelines carefully, as design failures have amplified negative impact on mobile.
- **Missing or Inadequate Mobile Design** — Many ecommerce sites lack a dedicated mobile design or fail to follow mobile-specific guidelines; some sites merely scale desktop designs to mobile screens, which does not adequately address mobile-specific interaction needs.
- **Tablet Performance** — Tablet users convert almost as well as desktop users (ratio of 1.18 vs. desktop 1.27), confirming that tablet usability is strong and needs less attention than mobile phone optimization.
- **Critical Role of Product Presentation** — On mobile screens showing only 3 products versus desktop's 6, getting product prioritization right is dramatically more important; product photos must be optimized for small thumbnails.

## Quotes

## Concepts

- [[Mobile Ecommerce]] — Online shopping via mobile devices; consistently generates lower revenue per visit than desktop due to platform constraints and often inadequate mobile-specific design implementation.
- [[Conversion]] — The percentage of visitors who complete a desired action (purchase); mobile conversion rates are substantially lower than desktop, indicating poor mobile user experience rather than user demographics.
- [[Responsive Design]] — Adapting a design across different screen sizes; naive responsive design (simply scaling content) does not adequately address mobile usability needs and contributes to poor mobile ecommerce performance.
- [[Platform Constraints]] — Inherent limitations of a technology platform; mobile's small screens and touch-based text input are fundamental constraints that demand mobile-specific design solutions beyond simple scaling.
- [[UX Metrics]] — Quantitative measures of user experience quality; revenue per visit is a more meaningful metric than conversion rate alone, accounting for both transaction frequency and purchase amount.
