# Designing Use-Case Prompt Suggestions

## Summary

Use-case prompt suggestions help users understand AI system capabilities and how to interact effectively. These examples range from simple single-word or short-phrase pills to complex conversation replays or video demonstrations, depending on system complexity, user familiarity with AI, and task complexity. Designers must balance two competing goals: helping new users quickly understand what the system can do, and teaching active users to leverage advanced capabilities.

For new users in pre-authentication views, simple, curated suggestions showcasing key capabilities are most effective. For active users, context-aware prompt suggestions appearing at the right moment—when users face ambiguity or lack domain knowledge—provide just-in-time guidance that reduces cognitive load and increases engagement. Analytics can help teams identify which suggestions drive engagement, though placement and order biases must be controlled for accurate optimization.

## Key Takeaways

- **Complexity depends on context** — simple pills suit broad systems and low-complexity tasks; rich examples suit specialized systems and high-complexity tasks.
- **Simple prompts drive initial adoption** — curated examples in pre-authentication views highlight capabilities clearly, functioning as lightweight onboarding that encourages account creation.
- **Context-awareness increases relevance** — prompt suggestions appearing when users face ambiguity or lack domain knowledge provide timely, specific guidance that reduces cognitive load.
- **Specificity beats vagueness** — targeted, concrete suggestions like "Easy family dinners" enable quick relevance assessment and drive engagement better than generic prompts like "Recipe ideas."
- **Individualization improves value** — suggestions tailored to user skill level, browsing behavior, or previous interactions feel more relevant and helpful than one-size-fits-all prompts.
- **Placement near input field maximizes engagement** — prompt suggestions positioned close to text input field capture users' attention at the moment they are most likely to engage.

## Quotes

> "Use-case prompt suggestions are examples of good AI prompts. They are displayed within most AI tools, usually (but not only) to novice users."

> "Simple, clickable prompts placed inside or near the input field work well for broad systems and low-complexity tasks."

> "By meeting users where they are in the experience and offering timely, specific, and meaningful prompts, designers can not only improve efficiency but also foster continued exploration and trust in the system."

## Concepts

- [[Prompt Engineering]] — creating effective examples and suggestions that guide users toward productive AI interactions.
- [[Onboarding]] — Interface techniques and content, including contextually appropriate assistance, help new users understand system capabilities and progress from initial confusion to confident, effective system use and adoption.
- [[Learnability]] — how easily users can understand what an AI system can do and how to interact with it effectively to achieve their goals.
