# Accordion Editing and Apple Picking: Early Generative-AI User Behaviors

## Summary

This article investigates two novel user behaviors that emerged in interactions with AI chatbots like ChatGPT. The researchers conducted qualitative usability studies with 8 professionals and students who use chatbots for work tasks, observing how users iterate with AI to refine outputs and manage the accumulation of chat history. The study reveals that while chatbots simplify the interface, they create new interaction challenges that current designs do not adequately support.

## Key Takeaways

- **Accordion Editing** — Users repeatedly ask AI to expand or shrink outputs to reach desired length and detail, similar to playing an accordion. Participants used word-count limits and forced ranking (asking for "top 5" items) to shrink responses and requested expansions to add detail to specific points.
- **Apple Picking** — Users reference specific elements from previous AI responses in new prompts to build on prior output, but the linear chat interface forces them to scroll extensively to locate and describe the parts they want to reference.
- **Excessive Scrolling Problem** — Long pages of accumulated chat require users to scroll through massive walls of text to find points they want to pick and reference, leading to users getting lost during navigation and comparison of iterations.
- **Editing Outside AI** — Most users still edit final outputs in external tools because ChatGPT struggles with real-world information, adjusting detail levels, and adding formatting.
- **UI Design Limitations** — The endlessly scrolling chat window offers poor support for iterative editing behaviors, contradicting claims that conversational UIs are inherently easier than traditional interfaces.
- **Need for Compartmentalization** — AI tools should allow direct editing of specific response portions rather than requiring new prompts for entire responses, implement point-to-select for referencing prior content, and support direct text editing without generating new output.

## Quotes

## Concepts

- [[User Behavior]] — Accordion editing and apple picking are new behaviors driven by limitations in current AI tools; the interface creates friction that shouldn't exist.
- [[Interaction Design]] — Conversational interfaces require specific design features like compartmentalization and point-to-select to support iterative work patterns.
- [[AI Chatbots]] — While simple on the surface, conversational interfaces create new usability problems that poorly designed chat histories do not adequately address.
