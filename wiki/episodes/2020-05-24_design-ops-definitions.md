# DesignOps: What's the Point? How Practitioners Define DesignOps Value

## Summary

Based on analysis of 341 DesignOps definitions collected from design practitioners through surveys and workshops, this article reveals six distinct mental models of DesignOps value. While 80% of definitions clustered into these six categories, the primary purposes practitioners cite are: standardizing processes (27%), enabling and supporting designers (22%), scaling design reach and impact (12%), increasing productivity (8%), enabling alignment and collaboration (6%), and producing better quality work (5%). The analysis shows that DesignOps is still an emerging field with low awareness—only 51% of survey respondents could provide a meaningful definition. Organizations benefit from thinking about DesignOps holistically rather than narrowly, as team needs evolve over time and require flexible, adaptable practices rather than single fixed models.

## Key Takeaways

- **DesignOps is defined by its intended goals, not fixed activities** — practitioners define DesignOps through the outcomes they want (standardization, support, scaling) rather than describing it as a set of concrete practices.
- **Standardization is the most common goal** — 27% of definitions cite establishing consistent processes, governance, and tool standards as DesignOps' primary value.
- **Enabling designers ranks second** — 22% focus on removing administrative burden so designers can focus on higher-value work, managing tools, and supporting team growth.
- **Scaling design impact requires systematic work** — 12% aim to grow the design discipline within organizations and systematically measure design influence on business outcomes.
- **Holistic framing is rare but valuable** — only 16 definitions framed DesignOps as comprehensive practice across multiple layers, yet this approach enables teams to adapt as needs shift.
- **DesignOps awareness remains low** — 49% of respondents couldn't provide meaningful definitions, suggesting DesignOps is still not widely understood despite growing focus in the field.
