# Growing in Your UX Career: Study Guide

## Summary

This article compiles Nielsen Norman Group's most useful resources for UX professionals at different career stages. Based on research with 722 UX professionals worldwide, it organizes guidance into sections for those considering a career transition into UX, those at entry level, and those at mid-level or senior levels. The resource addresses core questions like which path to choose within UX, how to build an effective portfolio, and how to advance through various career stages.

## Key Takeaways

- **UX careers are diverse** — practitioners come from varied backgrounds and bring numerous specialties, yet specific skills and responsibilities are recognized as crucial in the industry.
- **Career paths differ by stage** — resources are organized by career level (breaking in, entry level, mid and senior) because people enter UX at different life stages and need stage-appropriate guidance.
- **Portfolio presentation matters** — what hiring managers look for differs between designers and researchers, and between junior and senior positions, requiring tailored portfolio strategies.
- **Skills are learned progressively** — specific competencies like empathy, portfolio construction, and mentorship are developed through intentional practice and reflection.
- **Multiple resources support growth** — UX professionals can access videos, articles, and reports that address career-specific questions throughout their professional journey.
