# Are Your Personas Outdated? Know When It's Right To Revise

## Summary

Personas are snapshots in time of user behaviors, goals, and needs. As businesses evolve, technologies change, and user bases shift, personas risk becoming obsolete and misleading. Two primary signals indicate when revision is needed: changes in business and technology, and changes in user demographics or behaviors. Research data from analytics, user testing, and support calls can reveal these shifts. Teams that update personas quarterly to annually report higher satisfaction with design outcomes than teams that leave personas unchanged for five years or more.

## Key Takeaways

- **Monitor business and competitive changes** — if your product offerings, business model, or competitors' capabilities have shifted significantly, reassess whether personas still reflect your customer base.
- **Track demographic and behavioral shifts** — use analytics segmentation, user testing, and support data to detect whether users' actual behaviors, needs, and goals diverge from persona descriptions.
- **Update frequently for better outcomes** — teams updating personas quarterly reported higher impact ratings (5.5 on a 1-7 scale) than teams updating every 1-4 years (4.5) or rarely (3.9).
- **Communicate changes to the team** — when updating personas, explain what changed and why; ensure all team members have current versions and discard old copies to prevent confusion.
- **Avoid updating just for its own sake** — focus on identifying real shifts in user needs and business context rather than making changes without cause.
- **Personas left untouched for 5+ years are likely ineffective** — such outdated personas perform about as well as a dull butter knife cutting steak.

## Quotes

> Personas keep product-design decisions focused on user needs. They are snapshots in time of your users.

> Teams who keep their personas up-to-date perceive their projects as more successful in improving the user experience of their final products than teams who fail to keep persona data updated.

> If you have not updated your personas in 5 or more years, chances are they are performing for your products about as well as a dull butter knife cutting steak.
