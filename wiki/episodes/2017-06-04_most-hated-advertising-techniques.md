# The Most Hated Online Advertising Techniques

## Summary

This article presents a quantitative study of 452 US respondents rating 23 different ad formats on their level of annoyance (1-7 scale). Results show users dislike mobile ads more than desktop ads (5.45 vs 5.09), and modal ads, autoplay videos, intracontent ads that shuffle content, and deceptive links rank as most hated on desktop. On mobile, modal and intracontent ads with content reorganization are most disliked. Related links and sponsored social media content receive the least negative ratings. The study reveals users value control, predictability, and non-obstructive placement.

## Key Takeaways

- **Modal ads are universally disliked** — Modal advertisements that appear on top of content and require dismissal before interaction continues rank as one of the four most-hated ad types on both desktop and mobile.

- **Mobile ads frustrate users more** — The same ad format tested on both mobile and desktop consistently receives worse ratings on mobile, likely due to limited screen real estate and the mobile context of "on-the-go" usage.

- **Autoplay videos violate expectations** — Automatically playing video with audio is received negatively because it surprises users and takes control away; unskippable versions are even more disliked.

- **Related links perform best** — Content-adjacent links at the bottom of articles are the least-disliked ad format on both platforms, suggesting users accept ads that feel integrated and relevant to their task.

- **Control matters more than format** — Ads users can skip or dismiss receive more positive sentiment than forced interactions; perceived control is the primary driver of acceptance.

## Quotes

> The popups of the early 2000s have reincarnated as modal windows, and are hated just as viscerally today as they were over a decade ago.

> Ads that pop up in the way, force you to close them, or flash for attention just make me hate the product being advertised.

> With that in mind, what makes an ad annoying on desktop will make it intolerable on mobile.

## Concepts

- [[Advertising Design]] — quantitative analysis of user preferences for online ad formats, showing patterns of annoyance based on intrusiveness, control, and placement.
- [[Modal Window]] — modal ads identified as a primary source of user frustration across both platforms due to blocking content and forcing interaction.
- [[User Frustration]] — research documenting how ad characteristics like autoplay, forced dismissal, and lack of control drive negative affect and brand damage.
