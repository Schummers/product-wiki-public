# Design Guidelines for Selling Products with Multiple Variants

## Summary

Ecommerce sites must distinguish between product variations (different versions of the same product, like colors or sizes) and different products (fundamentally different items). When this distinction is not respected, usability suffers. Sites incorrectly representing different products as variants force users to compare complex options on a single page. Conversely, splitting variants across multiple product pages causes users to overlook variations and increases interaction complexity. This article provides guidelines: variants should be displayed under a single listing with selectable attributes, while different products should have separate listings. When variants are split across pages, listing pages must clearly signal the existence of variations through swatches or thumbnails.

## Key Takeaways

- **Different products need different listings; variants should use a single listing** with attribute selectors for colors, sizes, or patterns.
- **Attribute selectors on product pages should be reserved for simple variations**, not complex products that differ fundamentally.
- **Separate listings for variants cause discovery problems** because users must scroll past every variation of every product to find their desired item.
- **Users often don't realize they've saved duplicate variants** during browsing and face complex comparison during selection.
- **Listing pages must clearly signal availability of variants** through salient color swatches or thumbnails, not small "+MORE" links.
- **Multiple attributes (color and size) can be shown as combined swatches** or separate selections, depending on the number of combinations.

## Quotes

> Different products should have different listings; product variations should be displayed under a single listing(and variations should be presented as selectable attributes on the product detail page).

> It made it difficult to discover all product variations.

> For these reasons, attribute selectors on product pages should be reserved for product variations, which differ from each other only in that one attribute.
