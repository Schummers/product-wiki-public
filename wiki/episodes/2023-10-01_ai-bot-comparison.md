# ChatGPT, Bard, or Bing Chat? Differences Among 3 Generative-AI Bots

## Summary

Building on diary-study research with 18 users across three chatbots, this article compares ChatGPT, Bard, and Bing Chat on helpfulness and trustworthiness ratings. Bing Chat received significantly lower ratings than ChatGPT and Bard, primarily due to poor information aggregation and user-interface issues. The study demonstrates that UX design is critical to AI product success: features like references and suggested questions can help or hinder usability depending on execution quality.

## Key Takeaways

- **Bing Chat Rated Lower** — Bing Chat scored significantly lower on helpfulness and trustworthiness than ChatGPT and Bard, despite using the same underlying GPT model, highlighting that interface and information aggregation matter more than the model itself.
- **Poor Information Aggregation** — Bing Chat often provided shallow answers or directed users to links without synthesizing information, leaving users to do the work themselves rather than acting as a true information aggregator.
- **References as Double-Edged Sword** — While sources are valuable for verification, poorly curated or irrelevant references can reflect negatively on answer quality and reduce user trust.
- **In-Answer Links Require Accuracy** — Broken, outdated, or irrelevant links undermine credibility; both Bing Chat and Bard provided incorrect or nonexistent links that wasted user time.
- **Suggested Followup Questions Vary** — Questions that are too basic, too similar to the original query, or disappear after the next response frustrate users; however, well-designed followup questions help bridge the articulation barrier.
- **Multimedia Components Trade-Offs** — Videos and images can be helpful but create fear of losing context, may prevent quick scanning, and don't scale well on mobile devices where space is limited.
- **Ad Placement Matters** — Users accept contextually relevant ads in product-recommendation queries but find them annoying and offensive in research-oriented conversations.

## Concepts

- [[AI in Design]] — Chatbot quality depends heavily on information aggregation capability and interface design, not just the underlying language model.
- [[User Interface]] — Complex interfaces with many features can perform worse than simpler designs if features are poorly executed or create additional friction.
- [[Information Aggregation]] — True information aggregation means synthesizing multiple sources into a coherent answer, not simply pointing users to external links they must navigate themselves.
