# Orchestration in the Omnichannel Experience

## Summary

Customer journey orchestration is the surreptitious planning and coordination of a journey to minimize user effort for future actions. It involves predicting what customers will do next and meeting their specific needs before they formulate them. Three levels of orchestration exist: level one tailored to general user journeys through automation, level two tailored to large user segments using customer data, and level three using journey-orchestration engines powered by AI to deliver real-time personalization to granular segments.

## Key Takeaways

- **Definition of orchestration** — Orchestrating customer journeys means intelligently offering useful and relevant next steps because you understand your customer base well enough to predict potential needs or actions based on the current situation.
- **Level one orchestration** — Organizations can automate linear customer journeys by identifying common paths and removing effort required for the next step. This requires no customer data, only thoughtful service design. Example: Bellhops moving company sent contextually appropriate notifications as customers transitioned through journey steps.
- **Level two orchestration** — Uses specific customer data to facilitate personalized interactions based on segments. Organizations analyze customer journeys to identify opportunities to inject personalized interactions. Example: car-buying websites could send comparison emails or generate test-drive maps based on customers' browsing history.
- **Level three orchestration** — Uses journey-orchestration engines with customer-data platforms and AI capabilities to deliver real-time, personalized journeys. Software predicts future interactions and executes most relevant communications. Example: Uber sent food-delivery email after late-night ride based on user context and behavior patterns.
- **Maturity progression** — Organizations don't need advanced data infrastructure to achieve orchestration. Less complex approaches can begin with service design and progress to data-driven solutions as capabilities mature.
- **Monitoring effectiveness** — Organizations should monitor analytics and conversion rates for speculative nudges. If few customers accept suggestions, the nudge should be discontinued.

## Concepts

- [[Omnichannel Experience]] — Orchestration is one of five key attributes of high-quality omnichannel experiences, alongside consistency, context optimization, seamlessness, and collaboration across touchpoints.
- [[Customer Journey]] — Understanding common customer journeys and identifying moments to inject orchestrated interactions at the right time in the relevant context is central to effective journey design.
- [[Personalization]] — Three levels of orchestration provide pathways from simple automation to sophisticated AI-powered personalization that delivers unique pathways for each customer.
