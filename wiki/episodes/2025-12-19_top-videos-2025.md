# Top 10 UX Videos of 2025

## Summary

NN/g published its most-viewed videos from 2025, covering essential UX topics ranging from generalist skills and AI to fundamental design practices. The collection reflects what practitioners prioritized during the year: adaptability, AI integration, and foundational patterns. The most watched videos spanned AI's role in UX work, structural design approaches, and timeless usability concerns like button states and icon design.

## Key Takeaways

- **Return of the Generalist** — AI is making broad skills and adaptability more valuable than deep specialization, shifting career emphasis from narrow expertise to strategic thinking.
- **AI-Generated Interfaces (GenUI)** — Generative UI uses real-time AI to create personalized experiences, moving UX from static design to dynamic, user-specific interactions.
- **AI UX Intern Framework** — AI tools should be treated as first-pass contributors; designers must double-check outputs, verify facts, and provide specific instructions.
- **Disabled Buttons Hurt UX** — Disabled buttons confuse users by appearing clickable without response; designers should use them sparingly and explain clearly why they're disabled.
- **CARE Prompting Structure** — Writing better prompts for AI requires context, the ask, rules, and examples—a structured approach that yields higher-quality results.
- **Object-Oriented UX (OOUX)** — Identifying objects, their characteristics, and relationships simplifies designs and aligns with users' mental models for easier navigation.

## Quotes

> Broad skills, adaptability, and strategic thinking are becoming more valuable than deep specialization.

> To get better results from generative-AI chatbots, write CAREful prompts. Include context, what you're asking the system to do, rules for how to do it, and examples of what you want.

> Identifying objects, their characteristics, and relationships in an experience can help simplify designs and make systems easier to use by aligning with people's mental models.
