# UX Quiz: 2025 Year in Review

## Summary

This interactive quiz tests UX knowledge across ten dimensions: contextual menus, synthetic users versus digital twins, smart-home motivations, diary study design, mixed-methods research, AI trust, legacy users, AI-simulated participants, research workshops, and button states. The questions reflect key concepts covered in NN/g articles throughout 2025 and serve as both an assessment and a learning tool, with answer reveals connecting to deeper articles for each topic.

## Key Takeaways

- **Contextual Menus** — Secondary actions belong in contextual menus, not primary actions, and icons should be consistent across screens rather than tailored per layout.
- **Digital Twins vs. Synthetic Users** — Digital twins simulate specific individuals using rich, person-specific data; synthetic users simulate user segments using group-level descriptors.
- **Smart Home Motivation** — Research shows five core motivations: convenience, safety, resource/cost savings, environmental concern, and control—but not social status.
- **Diary Study Design** — Effective diary studies balance closed-ended, open-ended, and multimedia questions while prioritizing participant effort over maximizing free text.
- **AI and Trust** — Users trust AI systems that show clear, task-focused reasoning and limits; entertainment personas and false confidence undermine trust.
- **Button States vs. Styles** — Button styles indicate visual emphasis (primary vs. secondary); button states indicate interaction status (enabled, hover, pressed, disabled).

## Quotes
