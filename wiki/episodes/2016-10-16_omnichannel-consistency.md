# Consistency in the Omnichannel Experience

## Summary

Organizations using multiple channels (web, mobile, email, kiosks, chat, physical stores) must create consistent experiences where independent channel interactions coordinate into one cohesive customer experience. Consistency across three dimensions builds customer trust and efficiency: core functionality (supporting primary tasks on every channel with similar workflows), customer data (real-time, valid, consistent information across all channels requiring integrated backend systems), and visual design (cohesive visual story across channels signaling unified company). Inconsistency signals potential differences in offerings, functionality, or values, causing customers to question the organization's credibility and capabilities.

## Key Takeaways

- **Consistency is one of five omnichannel components** — Beyond consistency, omnichannel requires being optimized for context, seamless, orchestrated, and collaborative. Each contributes to the overall user experience.
- **Core functionality must work similarly across all channels** — Primary tasks should be supported on every channel with consistent workflows and interaction patterns. Though channel capabilities differ, consistency in feature behavior strengthens brand and builds on users' knowledge from prior interactions.
- **Customer data must be real-time and synchronized** — Backend systems must share and update data across channels so customers see consistent information as they move between touchpoints. Delays or inconsistencies force unnecessary contact and undermine confidence in organizational capabilities.
- **Visual design tells a unified brand story** — Consistent color palettes, imagery, typography, and visual elements across web, mobile, email, and packaging create the impression of a unified, integrated company. Extreme visual inconsistencies signal fragmentation and raise questions about whether offerings or values differ.
- **Different channel capabilities justify some inconsistency** — Though consistency matters, channels have different affordances. Optimizing for each channel's strengths and constraints may require designing differently while maintaining core consistency in primary workflows and brand presentation.
- **Consistency builds familiarity, learnability, efficiency, and trust** — Users expect consistent experiences, learn faster when interactions are familiar across channels, complete tasks more efficiently, and develop trust in companies that demonstrate integrated, cohesive operations.

## Quotes

> Creating consistency among these elements on every channel improves the customer experience as well as the brand image.

> Users crave consistency and companies that can provide consistent experiences across channels will quickly earn users' trust and build credibility.

> If the user experience isn't consistent across channels, users will question the organization's credibility.
