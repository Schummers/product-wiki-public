# Macro vs. Micro Conversions

## Summary

This article distinguishes between macro and micro conversions, helping product managers and digital marketers track user progress toward business goals. Macro conversions are user actions directly aligned with business objectives like purchases or lead submissions. Micro conversions are smaller steps along the user journey that indicate progress and can be measured more frequently. The article explains how to identify and track both types of conversions, with guidance for products with nonmonetary goals.

## Key Takeaways

- **Macro conversions align with business goals** — these are the primary user actions that directly contribute to an organization's main objectives, such as completed purchases or account creation.
- **Micro conversions measure incremental progress** — tracking smaller steps like adding items to a cart or visiting a checkout page provides more frequent feedback on design improvements than waiting for macro conversions.
- **Process milestones and secondary actions differ** — process milestones represent linear movement toward a macro conversion, while secondary actions build trust and loyalty without being required steps.
- **Mandatory vs. optional actions require different metrics** — for mandatory actions like intranet workflows, traditional conversion metrics are inappropriate; instead measure ease of use and error counts.
- **Nonmonetary products still track conversions** — even products without sales goals can track conversions for registration, document downloads, or other meaningful user actions when users have optional choices.

## Quotes

> A macro conversion is a desired user action that directly contributes to the primary goals of your business.

> Micro conversions are actions that are indirectly related to KPIs but that tend to happen more frequently.
