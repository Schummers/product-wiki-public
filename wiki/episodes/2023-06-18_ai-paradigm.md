# AI: First New UI Paradigm in 60 Years

## Summary

Jakob Nielsen argues that generative AI systems like ChatGPT represent the third major user-interface paradigm in computing history, following batch processing (1945) and command-based interaction (1964). This new paradigm, called intent-based outcome specification, shifts the user's role from issuing specific commands to describing desired outcomes and letting the AI determine how to achieve them. While current AI tools have significant usability problems and current chat interfaces are far from ideal, Nielsen predicts that hybrid interfaces combining intent-based AI with traditional GUI elements will likely dominate future computing.

## Key Takeaways

- **Three UI paradigms have dominated computing** — batch processing required submitting complete workflows to distant computers, command-based interaction enabled real-time feedback through turns between user and computer, and intent-based outcome specification allows users to specify desired results rather than precise steps.
- **Current AI tools have deep usability problems** — the rise of "prompt engineers" mirrors the need for specialized database query specialists before Google, suggesting that better UX design could give AI tools competitive advantage.
- **Literacy limitations affect AI usability** — based on recent literacy research, roughly half the population in wealthy countries may lack sufficient writing proficiency to get good results from current chat-based AI systems.
- **AI inverts control locus** — unlike command-based systems where users precisely direct actions, AI systems take control of execution, creating risk if users cannot verify the AI understood their intent correctly.
- **Hybrid interfaces will likely prevail** — future AI systems will retain GUI elements and visual interaction because text-based prose is less efficient than visual information for many tasks like form completion.
