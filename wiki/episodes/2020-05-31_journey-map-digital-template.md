# Journey Mapping for Remote Teams: A Digital Template

## Summary

Journey maps visualize customer goals, tasks, thoughts, emotions, and touchpoints across multiple phases of an experience, creating narratives that inform design. For remote and distributed teams, a spreadsheet template provides an accessible, collaborative alternative to specialized software. The template includes sections for persona, scenario, and goals; journey phases with user actions, thoughts, and emotions; insights and opportunities; and highs/lows analysis. This mid-fidelity approach is compatible with most organizations' tools (no special software or accounts needed), increases team ownership by using familiar interfaces, and serves as a first step toward higher-fidelity visualizations. Key advantages include no technical onboarding, universal accessibility across companies regardless of software policies, and the ability for teams to collaboratively maintain the artifact long-term.

## Key Takeaways

- **Spreadsheet templates democratize journey mapping** — using familiar spreadsheets reduces barriers to participation compared to specialized software, increasing team ownership and contribution.
- **Template structure aligns with journey map swim lanes** — columns represent journey phases while rows represent phases, actions, thoughts, emotions, insights, and highs/lows.
- **Compatibility enables widespread adoption** — most organizations allow spreadsheets due to data-protection policies, making this template accessible across different companies' technology landscapes.
- **No technical onboarding needed** — using familiar tools eliminates time spent learning software, allowing teams to focus immediately on content rather than interface.
- **Living artifacts require version control** — journey maps should be treated as evolving documents; version numbers and dates help teams understand that the artifact changes and may need updates.
- **Collaboration is as important as the artifact** — the process of building the map together is as valuable as the final output, informing design decisions more than the document alone.

## Quotes

> Journey maps visualize the process that a customer goes through to accomplish a goal.

> The process used to build the map is just as important as the map itself. Successful journey maps are not just about the resulting artifact and should be more treated as more than a checklist of the "right" elements.

> Don't be afraid to add another phase, expand a swim lane, or take any other liberties with the template provided.
