# Information Foraging with Generative AI: A Study of 3 Chatbots

## Summary

A two-week diary study following 18 users of generative-AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Google Bard, and Bing Chat) examined how people use these tools for information-seeking tasks. Participants logged 425 conversations, with 75% being information-seeking activities. The study found that chatbots dramatically reduce the interaction cost of information foraging by aggregating information from multiple sources, but users often trust results without verification and encounter pain points when bots provide vague answers or forget context.

## Key Takeaways

- **Shortcutting Information Foraging** — Generative AI bots eliminate the traditional three-step information foraging process (find, evaluate, aggregate) by presenting a single coherent answer, dramatically reducing user effort and interaction cost.
- **Helpfulness and Trustworthiness Scores** — On a 1–7 scale, bots averaged 5.77 for helpfulness and 6.00 for trustworthiness, though only 22% of conversations included verification, suggesting users trusted answers without checking.
- **Bot as Learning Instrument** — Bots helped users discover new information, correct misconceptions, and explore unfamiliar topics without time-consuming training, acting as bridges to understanding new domains.
- **Too Broad or Vague Information** — Users were disappointed when bots returned generic, high-level answers instead of specific, contextual responses; expectations for AI exceeded those for search engines because crafting prompts takes more effort.
- **Context Forgetting** — Bots sometimes forgot earlier context in conversations, providing irrelevant answers or searching for the wrong topic when users expected continuity.
- **Easy Verification Needed** — Checking answers requires significant interaction cost; tools should help users verify information quickly by providing sources or citations.

## Concepts

- [[Information Scent]] — Generative AI drastically reduces the effort required to find, evaluate, and aggregate information by automating the entire aggregation process.
- [[AI in Design]] — Chatbots provide efficiency gains for information seeking but require verification mechanisms and better handling of context to become fully trustworthy.
- [[User Trust]] — Users tend to trust AI outputs without verification because checking answers has high interaction cost; this creates risk when bots generate plausible-sounding but incorrect information.
