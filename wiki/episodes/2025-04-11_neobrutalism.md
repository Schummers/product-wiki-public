# Neobrutalism: Definition and Best Practices

## Summary

Neobrutalism is an emerging visual-design trend that combines bold, high-contrast colors, thick borders, stark shadows, and "unpolished" elements as a reaction against sleek minimalistic design. Evolving from traditional brutalism, neobrutalism incorporates nostalgic 90s graphic-design elements and is more colorful and orderly. Brands like Figma and Gumroad exemplify this aesthetic. The article emphasizes that while neobrutalism grabs attention, it must be balanced with usability and accessibility; designers must prioritize readability, contrast standards, whitespace, and clear interaction feedback to avoid overwhelming users.

## Key Takeaways

- **Neobrutalism defines itself through high contrast and rebellion against minimalism** — it uses bold primary colors, high-contrast combinations, thick borders, geometric shapes, stark drop shadows, and bold unconventional typography to create a striking, memorable visual impact.
- **Five core characteristics enable neobrutalist visual identity** — high contrast and bright colors emphasize key functions; thick lines and geometric shapes create structure without gradients; stark single-color drop shadows add depth; bold "unpolished" typefaces reinforce personality; skeuomorphic elements evoke nostalgic 90s and early-web aesthetics.
- **Usability must remain foreground in bold designs** — without balance, neobrutalism overwhelms users and hinders accessibility; priority must remain on clear buttons, readable type, ample whitespace, and intuitive interaction patterns even within raw aesthetics.
- **Accessibility standards still apply to bold aesthetics** — contrast ratios must meet WCAG standards; avoid pairing vibrant colors (yellow and cyan) that fail readability tests; tools like Coolors' contrast checker ensure combinations remain both striking and accessible.
- **Practical implementation requires constraint and hierarchy** — limit color palettes to 2-3 bold high-contrast colors; pair bold headlines with clean, neutral body fonts; use generous whitespace (24-32px margins) to offset geometric elements; maintain visual hierarchy through size variation and color intensity.

## Quotes
