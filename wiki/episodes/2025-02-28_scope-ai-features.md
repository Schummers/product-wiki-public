# Scope in Generative AI Features

## Summary

AI feature scope—how broad or narrow its capabilities—significantly impacts usability and user adoption. Broad-scope systems like ChatGPT and Perplexity accept diverse inputs and produce varied outputs, requiring users to understand vast capabilities and overcome articulation barriers. Narrow-scope AI features, like Spotify's playlist generator or Photoshop's generative fill, serve specific purposes with constrained inputs and outputs, making them easier for new users to understand and adopt. Usability research shows narrow-scope features outperform broad-scope systems for new users; the flexibility-usability tradeoff means additional functionality increases complexity and decreases usability. Scope decisions should arise from clear user and business needs, not from AI hype.

## Key Takeaways

- **Scope determines usability and user adoption** — Narrow-scope AI features perform better in usability studies; users find them easier to understand, generate appropriate prompts, iterate on outputs, and provide more-positive feedback than broad-scope systems.
- **Broad-scope systems require users to imagine capabilities** — ChatGPT's vast possibilities mean new users spend time exploring rather than accomplishing tasks; the system's flexibility creates complexity that new users must navigate without guidance.
- **Narrow scope enables guided interfaces and reduced cognitive load** — Narrowly scoped features like TripAdvisor's trip planner can use prompt wizards, helpful defaults, and structured outputs, reducing articulation burden and error risk compared to open-ended text fields.
- **Flexibility-usability tradeoff applies to AI as elsewhere** — More features mean more complexity; narrow scope paradoxically allows richer UI controls than broad scope, enabling more functionality discovery through guided interfaces.
- **Scope decisions should start with user and business needs** — Ask: What specific problem are we solving? How does it contribute to business goals? Is AI truly best? How clean is our data? Only after confirming fit should you decide scope.
- **Mental models evolve slowly across populations** — Younger users are adapting to AI-in-search patterns faster, but broad adaptation takes time; designing for current mental models while priming future expectations balances present usability with evolution.

## Quotes

> When designing an AI feature, its scope (how broad or narrow its capabilities are) influences its usability.

> A broad AI system's vast capabilities don't matter if users don't know they exist or don't know how to use them for that purpose.

> The more flexible a system is, the lower its usability will be.
