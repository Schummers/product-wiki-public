# Strategic Research: Advice from ResearchOps Expert Kate Towsey

## Summary

Kate Towsey, pioneering figure in research operations, explains that ResearchOps is fundamentally about delivering systems that enable people to do, consume, and act on research. She uses a civic-design analogy to illustrate how ResearchOps functions must interconnect within an organization's culture and practice. Rather than reactive "knee-jerk operations," successful ResearchOps requires strategic alignment with business goals, clear success metrics to secure leadership buy-in, and a measured approach to AI adoption that carefully evaluates where AI genuinely improves processes versus where human involvement remains essential.

## Key Takeaways

- **ResearchOps is a holistic system, not isolated tools** — Eight interconnected functions (participant recruitment, knowledge management, onboarding, tools, ethics, metrics, program management, and people development) operate within culture and practice; touching one function affects all, making comprehensive understanding critical.
- **Strategy must precede operations** — You cannot create effective operational systems without first clarifying what you want to achieve; business goals inform research strategy, which then determines what operational support and systems you need.
- **Start small and build over time** — Even if aiming for large-scale ResearchOps, begin with scoped pilot efforts and expand gradually; like building a city, you don't manifest Rome in one day, but thoughtful planning ensures coherent growth.
- **Communicate impact to secure buy-in** — Track metrics like number of participants recruited, reports produced, and people impacted by ResearchOps improvements; visibility at the right organizational level is essential for securing resources.
- **Approach AI thoughtfully as one of many tools** — While AI promises efficiency, teams must carefully evaluate where it genuinely smooths processes, enables automation, or takes over specific tasks versus where human judgment and context remain essential.
- **Expect organizational challenges during AI adoption** — Budget cuts, layoffs, and pressure to reduce headcount often precede proof of AI effectiveness; teams must maintain balanced perspective on AI's benefits while managing practical implementation challenges.

## Quotes

> The systems that enable people to do research, […] consume research, and act on research.

> If you touch one, you will end up working on them all. One of the key reasons that ResearchOps is often so misunderstood, so wildly underestimated in the amount of time it takes [...] is because this Venn diagram is not understood.

> Like all tools, you have to think about how it's used.
