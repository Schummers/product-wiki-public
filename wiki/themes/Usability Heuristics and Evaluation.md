# Usability Heuristics and Evaluation

## When to open this theme

Auditing an interface, running a heuristic evaluation or expert review, or scoring usability without users.

The audit theme: Nielsen's ten heuristics, cognitive walkthroughs, PURE scoring, competitive expert review. This is what a design review runs against.

## Concepts (9)

- [[User Experience]] (24 sources) : usability before delight, accumulated friction, and diagnosing an existing experience
- [[Usability Heuristics]] (15 sources) : Nielsen's ten, one sub-section each, and where they bend outside software
- [[Heuristic Evaluation]] (10 sources) : running an evaluation, expert review, cognitive walkthrough, and PURE scoring
- [[Benchmarking]] (10 sources) : getting a baseline before the work starts, choosing metrics, and the cheaper PURE instrument
- [[Usability]] (9 sources) : usability as a precondition rather than a finishing layer, and prioritising the problems found
- [[Aesthetic-Usability Effect]] (9 sources) : the instant judgement and what it decides, and countering it during research
- [[Error Prevention]] (8 sources) : designing for the inattentive user, confirmation dialogs used sparingly, defence in depth
- [[Learnability]] (7 sources) : the three aspects to distinguish, measuring it, and the cost of innovation and invisibility
- [[Design Principles]] (7 sources) : writing product-specific principles, and the visual-design principles the corpus uses
