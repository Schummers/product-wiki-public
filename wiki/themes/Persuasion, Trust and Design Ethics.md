# Persuasion, Trust and Design Ethics

## When to open this theme

Driving a behaviour, asking for data or permissions, building credibility, or checking whether a pattern has crossed into manipulation.

Holds the persuasion-versus-deception line explicitly, plus user control, customisation and privacy as trust mechanics.

## Concepts (8)

- [[Persuasive Design]] (16 sources) : the psychological levers, sequencing the ask to earned trust, and refusing deception
- [[User Trust]] (15 sources) : credibility signals, removing uncertainty in high-stakes purchases, calibrating trust in AI
- [[User Control]] (12 sources) : exits, undo, predictable dismissal, overriding system decisions, and where control has limits
- [[Privacy]] (10 sources) : the creepiness-convenience tradeoff, controls users expect, and consent in research
- [[Deceptive Patterns]] (9 sources) : the forms deception takes, telling persuasion from deception, and designing the honest version
- [[Personalization]] (8 sources) : whether to personalise at all, building the user model conservatively, presenting recommendations
- [[Customization]] (6 sources) : customisation versus personalisation, making it get used, and granular control against fatigue
- [[Behavioral Economics]] (5 sources) : the named biases, choice architecture in copy, and reducing sludge rather than adding nudges
