# Visual Design Craft

## When to open this theme

Typography, colour, grids, spacing, imagery, or judging whether a visual style choice costs usability.

The craft layer. Hierarchy and page-level composition sit in [[Page Composition and Hierarchy]]; system-level tokens in [[Design Systems and Consistency]].

## Concepts (9)

- [[Visual Design]] (45 sources) : principles, grids, type, colour, imagery, first impressions, and validating the result
- [[Emotional Design]] (13 sources) : building on usability rather than instead of it, surface versus deep delight, peaks and endings
- [[Typography]] (11 sources) : choosing and pairing typefaces, building hierarchy, spacing, and reading context
- [[Branding]] (9 sources) : brand as an experience system, defining one before there is a product, interaction qualities that carry it, and not looking like an ad
- [[Gestalt Principles]] (7 sources) : proximity, similarity, common region and closure, applied inside visual design
- [[Design Principles]] (7 sources) : writing product-specific principles, and the visual-design principles the corpus uses
- [[Design Trends]] (6 sources) : skeuomorphism, brutalism, neobrutalism and glassmorphism, and where each costs usability
- [[Animation]] (6 sources) : using motion for a purpose, timing, scroll-triggered motion, and motion sensitivity
- [[Flat Design]] (5 sources) : the evidence on weak signifiers, when fully flat is defensible, and risk reduction
