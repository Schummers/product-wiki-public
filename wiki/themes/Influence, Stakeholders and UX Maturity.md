# Influence, Stakeholders and UX Maturity

## When to open this theme

Getting buy-in, presenting to leadership, handling resistance, or raising a team's design maturity.

The political and organisational theme: stakeholder alignment, storytelling with evidence, change management, and proving business impact.

## Concepts (14)

- [[Stakeholder Engagement]] (64 sources) : aligning before the work starts, designing the meeting itself, listening before responding, showing the reasoning rather than the conclusion, locking in agreement, handling authority
- [[Decision Making]] (26 sources) : how users choose among alternatives, anchoring team decisions, auditing your own reasoning
- [[Business Impact]] (24 sources) : outcomes over outputs, leadership's language, and measuring the cost of a degraded experience
- [[Storytelling]] (22 sources) : what a UX story is made of, turning research data into narrative, and the grounding problem
- [[Design Presentation]] (22 sources) : knowing who is in the room, designing the meeting itself, choosing the message, responding to feedback, and closing the loop afterwards
- [[Organizational Culture]] (19 sources) : culture as a maturity factor, incentives, team structure, trust across disciplines
- [[UX Maturity]] (17 sources) : the four factors, what each stage looks like, moving up, and spotting regression
- [[User-Centered Design]] (16 sources) : personas and jobs-to-be-done, holding the balance with business, the organisational end state
- [[Design Leadership]] (16 sources) : leading the conversation rather than the decision, holding the line on changes without refusing them, and what the organisation owes design in return
- [[Design Critique]] (11 sources) : structuring the session, listening before responding, the receiving mindset, forming a response, keeping it on track, and closing the loop afterwards
- [[Change Management]] (10 sources) : mindset before operations, governance, actionable deliverables, and quiet maturity regression
- [[Active Listening]] (9 sources) : implicit and explicit listening with stakeholders, and reading a participant's comfort, tone and body language during a session
- [[DesignOps]] (8 sources) : the nine-activity menu, where to start, role versus mindset, measurement and maturity
- [[Design Advocacy]] (5 sources) : advocating a decision, pushing back on a bad suggestion without refusing it, advocating externally, and advocating for your own work
