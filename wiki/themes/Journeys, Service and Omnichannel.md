# Journeys, Service and Omnichannel

## When to open this theme

Designing across touchpoints, mapping a journey, blueprinting a service, or fixing handoffs between channels.

Zooms out past the screen to the whole delivery, backstage included. Personas and e-commerce journeys attach here.

## Concepts (10)

- [[Customer Journey]] (37 sources) : designing the journey rather than the touchpoint, mapping scope, and finding what breaks
- [[Persona]] (27 sources) : grounding them in research, segmenting by behaviour, and getting the organisation to use them
- [[Journey Mapping]] (25 sources) : one actor one scenario, the five-phase process, and not skipping the insights zone
- [[E-Commerce]] (22 sources) : listing pages, filtering, product information, cart and payment trust, post-order service
- [[Omnichannel Experience]] (19 sources) : mapping the ecosystem first, consistency, seamlessness, orchestration, the org condition
- [[Service Design]] (15 sources) : blueprinting as the core method, workshops, silos, and the whole delivery including physical
- [[Context of Use]] (10 sources) : mapping the when-and-where variables first, letting context pick the device, social context, and worked examples where it became the constraint
- [[Service Blueprinting]] (8 sources) : what it is for, the five-step process, scope, facilitation, and the root cause of failure
- [[Customer Experience]] (8 sources) : the three levels, journey mapping as shared view, and operationalising CX at scale
- [[User Flow]] (5 sources) : pairing flows with journeys instead of choosing, designing the branches rather than the happy path alone, the content each step needs, and reading competitors' flows in context
