# Adoption, Onboarding and Engagement

## When to open this theme

First-run experience, empty states, tutorials, activation, retention, or getting a feature actually used.

The recurring position: a learnable interface beats a dedicated onboarding flow, and onboarding is worth testing for necessity first.

## Concepts (6)

- [[Onboarding]] (19 sources) : testing whether onboarding is needed at all, contextual help over upfront tutorials, empty states
- [[Conversion]] (9 sources) : forms, speed, mobile, paywalls, exit moments, and not optimising the wrong metric
- [[Learnability]] (7 sources) : the three aspects to distinguish, measuring it, and the cost of innovation and invisibility
- [[User Engagement]] (7 sources) : frequency and recency over bounce rate, designing for return, the ethics of engagement
- [[User Retention]] (7 sources) : retention as the counterweight to conversion, NPS limits, and intervening at departure
- [[User Feedback]] (5 sources) : when and how to ask, and what qualitative feedback is good for and where it stops
