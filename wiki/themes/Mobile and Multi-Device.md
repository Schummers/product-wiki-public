# Mobile and Multi-Device

## When to open this theme

Designing for phone, tablet, watch, car screen, AR, or voice, and deciding what changes across screen sizes.

Mobile is treated as its own medium rather than a narrow desktop. Responsive mechanics, touch, microsessions and cross-device continuity all live here.

## Concepts (6)

- [[Mobile Design]] (49 sources) : mobile as its own medium: layout, touch, input, microsessions, content, and beyond the phone
- [[Discoverability]] (13 sources) : signifiers for invisible interactions, modes, and testing whether people find things
- [[Responsive Design]] (11 sources) : breakpoints, why porting is not adapting, images, tables, navigation across sizes
- [[Augmented Reality]] (7 sources) : whether AR adds value, realism and trust, discoverability, calibration, testing
- [[Voice Interfaces]] (6 sources) : discoverability without a screen, the cost of listening, and which tasks voice suits
- [[Smart Devices]] (5 sources) : whether to build at all, watch interactions, companion-app control, notifications as the core
