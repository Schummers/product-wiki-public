# Design Systems and Consistency

## When to open this theme

Building or governing a design system, naming components, or arguing about consistency versus one-off design.

Covers the system's contents, its semantics, and the organisational conditions that decide whether it gets adopted.

## Concepts (6)

- [[Design System]] (33 sources) : what it contains, semantics and naming, governance and adoption, and its cautions
- [[Documentation]] (16 sources) : knowing which document you are writing and for whom, and designing it for updating
- [[Typography]] (11 sources) : choosing and pairing typefaces, building hierarchy, spacing, and reading context
- [[Design Consistency]] (8 sources) : standard patterns as default, auditing by dimension, and encoding consistency in systems
- [[DesignOps]] (8 sources) : the nine-activity menu, where to start, role versus mindset, measurement and maturity
- [[Design Principles]] (7 sources) : writing product-specific principles, and the visual-design principles the corpus uses
