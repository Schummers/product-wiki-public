# Measurement, Metrics and Business Impact

## When to open this theme

Choosing metrics, setting a baseline, reading analytics, running an A/B test, or proving a design changed something.

Consistent warning across the corpus: start from goals, not from what the tool happens to report. When an A/B test is impractical (a small named enterprise user base, a change too large to split), [[Assumption Testing]] carries the alternatives.

## Concepts (10)

- [[Analytics]] (24 sources) : goals over what is easy to count, vanity metrics, site-search logs, connecting to business impact
- [[Business Impact]] (24 sources) : outcomes over outputs, leadership's language, and measuring the cost of a degraded experience
- [[UX Metrics]] (22 sources) : starting from goals, metric families, giving raw numbers context, baselines, reporting
- [[Product Outcome]] (12 sources) : which outcome a team should be given, Torres's anti-patterns, the outcome as the root of discovery, and how to measure it after release
- [[A-B Testing]] (10 sources) : where in a funnel to test, guardrail metrics, and what it cannot replace
- [[Benchmarking]] (10 sources) : getting a baseline before the work starts, choosing metrics, and the cheaper PURE instrument
- [[Conversion]] (9 sources) : forms, speed, mobile, paywalls, exit moments, and not optimising the wrong metric
- [[User Retention]] (7 sources) : retention as the counterweight to conversion, NPS limits, and intervening at departure
- [[User Engagement]] (7 sources) : frequency and recency over bounce rate, designing for return, the ethics of engagement
- [[Assumption Testing]] (7 sources) : surfacing and prioritising assumptions, designing a test that moves behaviour, and the twelve alternatives when an A/B test is not the tool
