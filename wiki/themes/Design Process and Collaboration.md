# Design Process and Collaboration

## When to open this theme

Structuring how the work gets done: process phases, workshops, ideation, prototyping, critique, or fitting design into Agile.

The 'how we work' theme. [[Story Mapping]] is here as a group activity and in [[Product Strategy and Framing]] as a planning artefact. Convincing people is a different problem, in [[Influence, Stakeholders and UX Maturity]].

## Concepts (17)

- [[Team Collaboration]] (84 sources) : workshops as default format, shared artifacts, critique, dissent, and remote collaboration
- [[Design Process]] (48 sources) : problem before solution, research woven through, prototyping and iteration, how rigid to be
- [[Workshop Facilitation]] (43 sources) : preparation, who is in the room, explain-execute-examine, dominance and groupthink
- [[Ideation]] (24 sources) : framing before generating, diverge then converge, breaking fixation, remote ideation, and finding a startup idea in the first place
- [[Problem Framing]] (22 sources) : outcomes before outputs, writing the problem and user-need statements, framing new technology
- [[Design Thinking]] (16 sources) : the four moves, it as a team practice, and where the sources push back on the framework
- [[Prototyping]] (14 sources) : starting from the hypothesis not the tool, choosing fidelity, Wizard of Oz, handoff
- [[Iterative Design]] (14 sources) : the loop and how many turns it takes, iterating in parallel, keeping fidelity low
- [[Agile Development]] (11 sources) : fitting UX into the backlog, scaling discovery instead of skipping it, quality debt
- [[Design Critique]] (11 sources) : structuring the session, listening before responding, the receiving mindset, forming a response, keeping it on track, and closing the loop afterwards
- [[Shared Understanding]] (10 sources) : why documents do not create it, what a team must talk about, mapping and modelling together, how many people and for how long, and the limit of the idea
- [[Product Trio]] (9 sources) : what the trio is for, assigning the outcome to it rather than to a person, working individually before merging, and where Torres and Patton describe different groupings
- [[Remote Work]] (8 sources) : team norms, synchronous versus asynchronous, and why familiar tools beat powerful ones
- [[Story Mapping]] (8 sources) : the two axes and the backbone, slicing a map into releases, the now map versus the later map, and running the mapping as a group rather than drawing it alone
- [[Team Scaling]] (6 sources) : scaling research through operations, a design system without more people, and a studio scaling authority without a management layer
- [[Productivity]] (5 sources) : leverage over hours, protecting the conditions to ship, fixing the process rather than only your own habits, tooling, and designing for users who switch tasks
- [[Wireframing]] (5 sources) : no drawing skill needed, why messy is the point, paper before the tool, wireframes as the story of a page, and Penchenat's argument that the era is over
