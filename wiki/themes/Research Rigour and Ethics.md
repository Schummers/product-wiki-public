# Research Rigour and Ethics

## When to open this theme

Sample size, recruiting, screeners, validity, bias, consent, or defending a study someone is challenging.

The quality half of research. Open it once the method is chosen and the question is whether the study will hold up.

## Concepts (13)

- [[Participant Recruitment]] (22 sources) : screeners, where to find people, over-recruiting against no-shows, hard-to-reach groups
- [[Data Analysis]] (19 sources) : assessing data before interpreting it, thematic coding, affinity diagramming, statistical care
- [[Study Design]] (16 sources) : protecting internal and external validity, order effects, attrition, and observer effects
- [[Research Planning]] (15 sources) : what a plan contains, piloting, session length, and planning for a protocol that will change
- [[Research Validity]] (12 sources) : internal versus external validity, sampling, and saying so when validity is sacrificed
- [[Research Ethics]] (12 sources) : informed consent, deception and debriefing, vulnerable populations, data across the lifecycle
- [[Research Operations]] (11 sources) : panels, repositories, governance, standardisation, and why the work gets underestimated
- [[Privacy]] (10 sources) : the creepiness-convenience tradeoff, controls users expect, and consent in research
- [[Bias in Research]] (9 sources) : how recruiting, screening, task wording and moderation each inject bias
- [[Young Users]] (8 sources) : physical and cognitive development, teenagers, and consent and assent with minors
- [[AI in User Research]] (8 sources) : where AI helps, where it fails, and the open disagreements between sources
- [[Sample Size]] (5 sources) : where 'five users' comes from and when it breaks, plus quantitative sizing and saturation
- [[Confirmation Bias]] (5 sources) : investigating rather than validating, and triangulating against your own expectations
