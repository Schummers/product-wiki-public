# Venture Building and Studios

## When to open this theme

Building or joining a venture studio, validating ideas in batches, studio equity and economics, recruiting founders, raising for a studio, or founding a startup yourself.

The one theme sourced from books rather than articles (Szigeti 2019, Kannan and Peterman 2022, Hexa 2026; docs/adr/0005). Two of the three books look at company building from the studio's seat, the third from the founder's, which is where the sharp edges on cofounders, first hires, design partners and go-to-market come from. It also carries the product concepts all three feed (PMF, MVP, strategy). No design review runs against it; the books disagree on numbers often enough that every page keeps both.

## Concepts (17)

- [[Venture Studio]] (27 sources) : what a studio does that other models do not, where ideas come from, the platform, founders and equity, and where the two books disagree
- [[Studio Economics]] (18 sources) : front-loaded costs and late returns, how to build the model, unit costs, intermediate revenue, exits and the best-in-class case
- [[Equity Ownership]] (13 sources) : why a studio takes founding equity, how much (the books differ), dilution, ownership floors, common versus preferred
- [[Venture Capital]] (11 sources) : how the studio model differs from VC, VC as investor in the studio, keeping ventures fundable, borrowing the fund structure
- [[Studio Fundraising]] (11 sources) : funding the operating company versus raising a fund, the credibility barrier, closed-ended or evergreen, revenue as a substitute for capital
- [[Minimum Viable Product]] (10 sources) : aim small and ship, prototype before the MVP, the MVP as a funnel step, viable versus loveable, one percent of the vision shipped in weeks
- [[Business Strategy]] (10 sources) : diagnosis, guiding line and action plan, vision statements linking strategy to UX, planning a studio like a factory, and whether execution beats the idea (the books disagree)
- [[Founder Recruitment]] (9 sources) : what a studio offers a founder, who it looks for, sourcing models, cash and equity compensation, bringing founders in early
- [[Portfolio Strategy]] (9 sources) : the portfolio as risk-management unit, how many ventures at what cost, market focus (where the books diverge), governance and killing
- [[Idea Validation]] (9 sources) : why validate before building, methods including Hexa's interviews-writing-wireframes sequence, running ideas in parallel, time and budget, deciding and killing
- [[Product-Market Fit]] (8 sources) : confronting the market early, tools and cold calls to estimate fit, fit as a studio gate, design partners and one focus metric as early signals, timing, keeping fit once shipped
- [[Studio Funnel]] (7 sources) : stage structures, filling the top, scoring and budgeting, conversion rates, throughput, modelling the funnel
- [[Revenue Model]] (6 sources) : the cash-flow gap, billing back launched companies, corporate co-builds and consulting, agency revenue, spending less instead
- [[Innovation]] (6 sources) : three scales: deviating from an interface standard, innovating in at least one dimension of an idea, and innovation as a studio's engine with its limits
- [[Team Scaling]] (6 sources) : scaling research through operations, a design system without more people, and a studio scaling authority without a management layer
- [[Team Compensation]] (6 sources) : salary and equity in the early team, the concrete numbers (ESOP size, vesting, salary grids), founder equity splits, and where the payroll money comes from
- [[Studio Team]] (5 sources) : which competencies to hold in-house, structuring the team across ventures, founding composition, attracting and retaining talent
