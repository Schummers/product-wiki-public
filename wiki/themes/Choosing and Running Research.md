# Choosing and Running Research

## When to open this theme

Picking a research method, running a usability test, writing tasks, or deciding what evidence a question actually needs.

The entry point for 'how do I find out'. Method selection lives here; rigour, recruitment and ethics live in [[Research Rigour and Ethics]].

- [[Research Methods]] (98 sources) : the master inventory: how to choose among methods, and what each one can and cannot answer

- [[A-B Testing]] (10 sources) : where in a funnel to test, guardrail metrics, and what it cannot replace
