# Product Backlog

## Definition

_Stub — to be written by the concept stage._

## Sources (3)

- [[2014-09-05_user-story-mapping_06-1-the-big-picture]] — Patton critiques the traditional flat, prioritized backlog as a flawed starting point; story mapping serves as an alternative structure that makes the product's shape visible and helps teams minimize scope.
- [[2014-09-05_user-story-mapping_11-6-the-real-story-about-stories]] — The collection of story cards describing things users want to do or changes to make; the term itself reflects the legacy of treating stories as items to be managed, though the real value comes from conversations.
- [[2014-09-05_user-story-mapping_22-17-stories-are-actually-like-asteroids]] — Large backlogs become unmanageable when filled with tiny stories. Patton recommends bundling small items under distilled headers, treating the bundled cards as the working backlog and original stories as supporting details, which makes prioritization and context easier.
