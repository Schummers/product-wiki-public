## Sources (4)

- [[2019-06-30_popups]] — overlays range from modal (blocking) to nonmodal (allowing background interaction) and from dimmed (lightbox) to transparent; nonmodal, non-dimmed overlays are less intrusive and often more effective for non-critical messaging.
- [[2021-08-01_overlay-overload]] — Overlays are content boxes displayed on top of another page; they range from non-modal (can be ignored) to modal (require explicit interaction); while effective at capturing attention, they are frequently perceived as disruptive and annoying.
- [[2022-09-18_accidental-overlay-dismissal]] — Overlays are useful for maintaining context but create inherent risks; best practices include avoiding overlays, using partial overlays, preventing stacks, providing clear close buttons, and supporting back buttons.
