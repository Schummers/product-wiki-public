## Sources (4)

- [[2026-03-05_10x-method_01-becoming-a-10x-founder]] — The ten traits Hexa identified (vision, adaptability, execution, rituals, low ego, team building, global thinking, mission focus, resilience, kindness) are the DNA of a 10x founder and can be learned and developed over time. Purpose connected to the mission is what fuels resilience: missionary founders driven by impact rather than exit timelines keep the energy and clarity to persist through chaos.
- [[2026-03-05_10x-method_05-hiring-early-talent]] — Early hires must be builders, not task-doers; highly adaptable, deeply autonomous, and entrepreneurial. They should already know how to operate in fast-moving environments, solve problems independently, and contribute from day one. Assess entrepreneurial mindset through structured questions about what they're exploring, why they're looking, and how they'd decide between offers; look for people whose inner motivation aligns with building something new, not chasing prestige or compensation alone.
- [[2026-03-05_10x-method_06-getting-your-first-customers]] — The founder's personal involvement in early sales, content, and brand-building is essential. Prospects invest in the founder and team as much as the product; founders should personally lead sales until at least $1M ARR and close the first $50K themselves to internalize what works.
- [[2026-03-05_10x-method_07-brand-and-comms]] — A founder's personal brand and story are among the company's most valuable early assets. Authentic presence on one primary channel (LinkedIn, Twitter, blogging, podcasting) built through consistent effort compounds over time. The emotional core of your founder story should connect to your company's brand feeling while remaining genuine.
