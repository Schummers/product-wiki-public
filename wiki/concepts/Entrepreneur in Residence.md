# Entrepreneur in Residence

_Stub — to be written by the concept stage._

## Sources (4)

- [[2019-02-17_startup-studio-playbook_05-emerging-from-beta]] — Formalized through programs like Hackers-in-Residence, where experienced builders explore product ideas within the studio's protected environment before committing to full venture development.
- [[2019-02-17_startup-studio-playbook_17-entrepreneurs-in-residence-and-ceos]] — EIRs are essential to studio scaling; they combine entrepreneurial drive with flexibility to move between ventures, discover market needs via lean validation, lead teams, generate sales traction, and navigate fundraising; studios recruit on track record, leadership style, market network, and coachability.
- [[2022-02-08_venture-studios-demystified_08-working-with-founders]] — EIRs are founders and operators in various stages of engagement with studios—from short-term participants in accelerator-like programs (3 months, minimal equity) to long-term apprentices working on multiple studio ventures before leading their own. Programs range from $150,000+ salaries in major markets to term-based contractor stipends ($50k–$150k+ over 6–12 months).
- [[2022-02-08_venture-studios-demystified_21-other-assumptions]] — details varying EIR program structures, including success rates (3-6%), duration (3-6 months), compensation models (salary draw or cost-sharing), and the variability of EIR-led idea sources across studios.
