# Early Hiring

## Sources (2)

- [[2026-03-05_10x-method_05-hiring-early-talent]] — Early employees set the company's standard, culture, and execution rhythm. The first 10 hires warrant the same rigor as cofounder selection because their impact shapes everything downstream: product quality, customer acquisition, brand, and retention. Founders must spend 30–40% of their time on recruitment, understanding the hiring process firsthand before delegating it, and moving fast to close candidates (time-to-hire should be 2–3 weeks, time-to-fill under 3 months).
- [[2026-03-05_10x-method_06-getting-your-first-customers]] — Before hiring growth specialists or salespeople, founders must prove they can sell and understand GTM mechanics. This first-hand knowledge directly informs hiring decisions and allows founders to recognize when a new hire will excel or struggle in the role.
