- [[2026-03-05_10x-method_03-choosing-your-cofounder]] — Regular rituals (weekly check-ins, quarterly value realignment, weekly self-assessments where each cofounder shares what went well, what could improve, feedback needs, and worries) create intentional moments of communication that prevent misalignment from developing silently and maintain relationship health under pressure.
- [[2026-03-05_10x-method_04-building-your-mvp]] — Weekly product committees (30–60 minutes, same time each week) and quarterly milestone planning (1–2 hours) establish rhythm for execution. Weekly sessions demo progress, refine priorities, and address blockers; quarterly sessions reset milestones and prevent day-to-day work from derailing long-term vision. These rituals are crucial even at two-person founding teams and scale naturally as the company grows.
- [[2026-03-05_10x-method_05-hiring-early-talent]] — Weekly one-on-one conversations (30–60 minutes) track progress, surface blockers, and address challenges; monthly development meetings zoom out to assess career goals and co-create development plans. Onboarding milestones anchor integration (week one), feedback exchange (month one), and go-no-go decisions (month three). These routines keep performance and growth on track, embed feedback as a habit, and scale naturally as the team grows.
