## Sources (2)

- [[2019-12-17_storytelling-in-design_04-chapter-3-storytelling-for-product-design]] — The chapter covers how choose-your-own-adventure (CYOA) and interactive media allow audiences to "influence the characters' decisions and thereby the way the story developed." Dahlström cites *Mosaic* and user desire for agency, then notes the nuance: co-creation can often shorten the production process, but in instances like the AXE Anarchy campaign (users' choices turned into a comic book by the agency Razorfish) it can also take longer, and *Mosaic*'s reviews were mixed, some finding the storyline confusing and the plot easy to miss bits of.
- [[2019-12-17_storytelling-in-design_13-chapter-12-choose-your-own-adventure-stories-and-modular]] — CYOA stories and gamebooks exemplify user-driven, choice-based narratives with multiple endpoints. Product experiences increasingly resemble this model as users control their entry points, progression, and exits through branching CTAs and personalized content.
