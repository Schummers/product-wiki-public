## Sources (2)

- [[2026-03-05_10x-method_04-building-your-mvp]] — Design partner programs transform early users from passive testers into active co-builders, embedded in your product development with daily conversations, iterated prototypes, and real-world behavior observation. This approach reveals whether users truly need your product (not just find it interesting) and serves as early validation that product-market fit is achievable.
- [[2026-03-05_10x-method_06-getting-your-first-customers]] — Design partners are the bridge from MVP validation to first revenue. They pay a meaningful fee, receive early access and iteration, and provide structured feedback via weekly check-ins and monthly workshops. A well-run 3-month program typically converts 80% of partners to long-term customers.
