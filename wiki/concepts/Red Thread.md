# Red Thread

## Definition

_Stub — to be written by the concept stage._

## Sources (4)

- [[2019-12-17_storytelling-in-design_02-chapter-1-why-storytelling-matters]] — Introduced as the Swedish concept *röd tråd*, this is the unifying theme or common thread that runs through a story, connecting all events and details into a coherent whole; Dahlström emphasizes that without this thread, a story lacks the dynamic structure that holds an audience.
- [[2019-12-17_storytelling-in-design_10-chapter-9-visualizing-the-shape-of-your-product-experience]] — Dahlström stresses that experience goals create a "through line" running through the entire product, ensuring that at any point in the experience, designers can articulate how that moment translates as an emotional feeling; this alignment is crucial for both teams and stakeholders.
- [[2019-12-17_storytelling-in-design_12-chapter-11-theme-and-story-development-in-product-design]] — the through-line that binds product design together; established by a clear theme and ensures that all content, features, and interactions are connected and purposeful.
- [[2019-12-17_storytelling-in-design_13-chapter-12-choose-your-own-adventure-stories-and-modular]] — The through-line that connects all branches must remain clear. Even as users take different paths, the core promise and theme of the product experience should persist, just as in Gauntlet-structure CYOA stories where one central narrative thread holds the branching together.
