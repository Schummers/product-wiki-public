## UX Careers: An Overview

This compilation of resources is geared toward existing UX practitioners and those considering a career transition into UX. It’s meant to help you with career-related questions, such as which path to choose within UX or what to include in a research or design portfolio.

As people come to UX at very different stages of their lives and from different roles, the resources are divided by career levels ([breaking-in](#breaking), [entry-level](#entry), and [mid-level or senior](#midsenior)), so you can jump to the content that applies to you. The first section below is dedicated to general UX-career advice and ways to present your work when applying for user-experience positions.
