  1. Put primary actions like *Reply* and *New message* only in contextual menus to reduce clutter.
  2. Use contextual menus to store secondary actions related to a specific item.

  4. Use a different icon for contextual menus on every screen to match that screen’s layout.

  1. Synthetic users and digital twins are the same thing; the terms are interchangeable.
  2. Synthetic users are AI models of specific individuals; digital twins are generic simulations of a user group.
  3. Synthetic users simulate a user segment using group-level descriptors, while digital twins simulate a particular individual using rich, person-specific data.
  4. Synthetic users are based only on past behavioral logs, while digital twins are based only on demographic information.

4. **You’re planning a diary study. Which of the following is a best practice for designing diary-study entries?**

  1. Maximize open-ended questions; more free text always means better data.
  2. Avoid rating scales because they’re too “quantitative” for diary studies.
  3. Balance closed, open-ended, and multimedia questions while prioritizing participant effort.
  4. Ask participants to upload a video for every entry to capture rich context.

  1. Triangulation error

  3. Multivariate testing
  4. Benchmarking

6. **A team is building an AI assistant for work tasks. The team wants users to trust the AI assistant. Which approach is most effective, according to research?**

  1. Give the AI an entertaining persona, with jokes and emotional backstories.
  2. Emphasize the AI’s analytical capabilities and show clear, task-focused reasoning and limits.
  3. Hide uncertainty and always speak confidently, even when the AI is unsure.
  4. Add emojis and friendly small talk so people feel like they’re chatting with a colleague.

Reveal answer

  3. Long-time users with deep familiarity but who rely on awkward workarounds
  4. Long-time users of a similar app who are switching to this app

8. **A team is exploring AI-simulated users (digital twins or synthetic users) to support UX research. Which statement is most accurate?**

  1. AI-simulated users can fully replace research with real people once the model is trained.
  2. Models built from rich, individual-level data tend to be more accurate than models based only on generic demographics.
  3. Synthetic users always precisely predict both behavior trends and exact effect sizes.
  4. Digital twins automatically eliminate bias because they use objective AI.

Reveal answer
9. **You’ve completed a large multi-country study and you’ve shared results with your team. Now you want a hands-on session that helps the team translate insights into concrete opportunities and design directions. Which type of research workshop should you use?**

  1. Research-alignment workshop
  2. Empathy workshop
  3. Research-application workshop
  4. Brainstorming sprint with no research materials

Reveal answer
10. **What is the difference between button state and button style?**

  1. Button state is about color only; button style is everything else.
  2. Button styles indicate visual emphasis (e.g., primary vs. secondary), while button states indicate interaction status (e.g., enabled, hover, pressed).
  3. Button styles are temporary; button states are permanent.
  4. Button styles are for desktop; button states are for mobile.
