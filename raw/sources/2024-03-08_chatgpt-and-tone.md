Many writers turn to ChatGPT or other AI chatbots to edit and perfect their tone. Much like people, however, **AI struggles to convey nuanced emotions**. Some **AI users have suggested tips that produce better text and mitigate AI’s problems.** But how effective are they? My research found that **using existing copy and asking for multiple responses created natural outputs**. Using tone words, on the other hand, didn’t.

## Include Tone Words in the Prompt

### One Tone Word Works Poorly

Using a [tone-of-voice word](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tone-voice-words/) in your prompt is probably the most natural way to elicit a particular tone for the output text.

> *Prompt:* Write a press release for Duke Hospital with a formal tone. [*Details*]

While this is the easiest and most uncomplicated approach, it isn’t one I suggest. ChatGPT tends to “latch” onto the adjective and exaggerate it unnaturally.

> *Prompt:*
> 
> 
> 
> Rewrite this mission statement in a happy tone:
> 
> 
> 
> Google’s mission is to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful. That's why Search makes it easy to discover a broad range of information from a wide variety of sources.

> *Response:*
> 
> 
> 
> Google is on a delightful journey to arrange the world's information, aiming to make it universally accessible and incredibly helpful. This is where the magic of Search comes in, making it a breeze to uncover an array of information from diverse sources.

In this example, ChatGPT delivers a revised mission statement that feels forced and unnatural in its extreme exuberance. The response text may be more appropriate for Disney than Google. This is particularly noticeable in the word choice:

- “delightful”
- “cheerful”
- “bright”
- “magic”

Even when provided with a more nuanced tone descriptor, like “light-hearted,” “ambitious,” “vibrant,” or “nurturing,” ChatGPT can still exaggerate.

> *Prompt:*
> 
> 
> 
> Rewrite this warranty advertisement in a nurturing tone:
> 
> 
> 
> Sometimes life requires a little maintenance. When your appliance does too, use Whirlpool replacement parts designed specifically for your needs. Our factory certified Whirlpool parts provide you with a promise only Whirlpool brand can make — that through our safe and genuine quality replacement parts, we will care for your appliance like you care for your home every day.

> *Response:*
> 
> 
> 
> In the gentle unfolding of life, there are moments when a touch of care is needed, not just for ourselves but for the spaces we hold dear. When the appliances that serve as the heartbeat of your home whisper for attention, embrace the warmth of Whirlpool replacement parts, each crafted with your needs in mind. With our factory-certified parts, we extend to you a promise only the Whirlpool family can offer — a commitment to envelop your appliance in safety and genuine quality. Just as you nurture your home with love and dedication each day, we vow to tenderly care for the tools that help make your house a home.

#### Celebrity Tone Word

In this same vein, some AI users suggest using a famous writer or other celebrity in the prompt to request output that resembles the tone that person uses in writing or speech. (This method is the equivalent of including the name of [a famous visual artist in the prompt when generating an image](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-articulation-barrier/).)

Depending on the person chosen, this method might generate more-conversational copy but will run into the same issues as before. So, **I don’t recommend this tactic for web copy**.

### Multiple Tone Words: Better than One

The adjective method **works better if you use multiple descriptors in one prompt**.

> *Prompt:*
> 
> 
> 
> Rewrite the following in a happy, professional, business-casual tone:
> 
> 
> 
> Google’s mission is to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful. That's why Search makes it easy to discover a broad range of information from a wide variety of sources.

> *Response:*
> 
> 
> 
> At Google, we're excited to bring the world's information to your fingertips in an organized, accessible, and incredibly useful way. With our Search function, finding a diverse array of information from numerous sources is not just simple, it's a delightful experience.

Using multiple adjectives prevents ChatGPT from latching onto a specific word, creating a more natural and conversational response. While not perfect, this new response does feel a bit more human.

With a few edits, you can make this over-the-top copy just a bit more down-to-earth.

> *Human Edited:*
> 
> 
> 
> At Google, we're excited to bring the world's information to your fingertips in an organized, accessible, and incredibly useful way. With our Search function, finding a diverse array of information from numerous sources is not just simple, it's a delightful experience.

While it might require more editing than some of the others on this list, using tone words can be helpful – especially when you’re experimenting with different tones.

### Tips

- **Using tone words can be a great place to start,** but ChatGPT tends to latch on to tone words and take them too far.
- **ChatGPT will often exaggerate certain tones** in a prompt, creating unnatural responses.
- **Using multiple, nuanced tone** words will create the best results.
- **Don’t forget to edit** the text to make the text feel more human and less cartoony.
- **Avoid using celebrities** in the place of tone words.

## Use Existing Copy to Train the AI Chatbot

Using sample copy to train ChatGPT achieved even better results than a set of nuanced tone words.

After seeing an example, ChatGPT quickly mirrored the existing style and tone. The longer prompts also helped avoid some of the exaggerations of previous examples, as the AI had more to focus on and replicate.

> *Prompt:*
> 
> 
> 
> Take the tone of this copy:
> 
> 
> 
> Mailchimp uses generative AI to automate manual marketing tasks—such as writing a first draft or visualizing data. This can help you boost customer engagement and drive higher conversion.
> 
> 
> 
> And use it to revise:
> 
> 
> 
> Plaid uses advanced machine learning to help businesses make better risk decisions, improve the customer experience, and fight fraud at every stage of the customer journey.

> *Response:*
> 
> 
> 
> Plaid leverages the power of advanced machine learning to streamline your business operations, enhancing risk management, elevating the customer experience, and combating fraud throughout the customer journey. This innovative approach not only secures your transactions but also paves the way for stronger customer connections and improved conversion rates.

To create the results from the Mailchimp/Plaid example, I first fed ChatGPT an example from an AI ad and then asked it to edit another company’s version. The AI quickly adjusted the slightly serious tone of Plaid’s copy, aligning it with Mailchimp’s brighter tone. The response was still business casual and cheery but felt natural.

If your organization doesn’t already have tone-of-voice guidelines, consider using ChatGPT to help you define them. Ask the AI to analyze or describe your tone from an ideal piece of text.

> *Prompt:*
> 
> 
> 
> *Analyze this tone:*
> 
> 
> 
> We source fabric and clothes that nobody’s using and make them into cute, new stuff for you. Deadstock is leftover fabric from other designers and reworked vintage is old stuff from wholesalers. Remanufactured clothing can save more than 13,000 pounds of CO2 emissions per person, per year, plus it may or may not have been worn by Cher. We also source lots of one- of-a-kind pieces for Ref Vintage."

> *Response:*
> 
> 
> 
> The tone of the text in the image is casual and conversational. It aims to be relatable and speaks directly to the reader in a friendly manner. The use of the word "cute" and the playful hypothetical about clothes possibly worn by Cher adds a lighthearted and whimsical touch. The text also has an informative edge, as it explains the environmental benefits of choosing their products, suggesting a responsible and eco-conscious brand personality.

Now, with only a few edits (or a request to ChatGPT), this response can be used to guide yourself, colleagues, or ChatGPT when writing or editing new content.

> *Prompt:*
> 
> 
> 
> Using the previous description, write a tone guide for a company.

> *Response:*
> 
> 
> 
> Based on the description provided for the tone of the text in the image, here's a tone guide that a company might use for their communications:
> 
> 
> 
> Company Tone Guide
> 
> 
> 
> Our brand voice is the essence of our company's personality, and the tone we use in our communications should consistently reflect this personality. It's important to strike the right balance between being casual and informative, ensuring we are both engaging and responsible in our messaging *.*

### Tips

- **Use existing copy** to train ChatGPT.
- **Use the same author or type** of writing when possible.
- **Ask ChatGPT to analyze your tone** and save the response for future guidance and prompt engineering.

## Ask for Alternative Outputs

**Sometimes it takes a few tries for AI tools like ChatGPT to write exactly what you’re looking for**. By requesting multiple answers, you’ll increase the likelihood of finding something useful.

I asked ChatGPT to provide 5 approachable-style alternatives for the copy advertising the features of a video game.

> *Prompt:*
> 
> 
> 
> Give me 5 alternatives to the following copy in an approachable style:
> 
> 
> 
> Experience lightning-fast loading with an ultra-high speed SSD, deeper immersion with support for haptic feedback1, adaptive triggers1 and 3D Audio*, and an all-new generation of incredible PlayStation games.

> *Response*:
> 
> 
> 
> 1. Dive into the future of gaming with our ultra-fast SSD, ensuring your games load in the blink of an eye. Feel the game like never before with haptic feedback, adaptive triggers, and 3D Audio, all while exploring a breathtaking new lineup of PlayStation games.
> 
> 
> 
> 2. Get ready to be whisked away into gaming paradise with lightning-quick load times thanks to our ultra-high speed SSD. Immerse yourself deeper with life-like haptic feedback, responsive adaptive triggers, and enveloping 3D Audio, accompanied by an extraordinary collection of new PlayStation games.
> 
> 
> 
> 3. Step into a new era of gaming with our ultra-fast SSD, designed to catapult you into action in seconds. Experience gaming with heightened realism through haptic feedback, adaptive triggers, and 3D Audio, and lose yourself in an awe-inspiring selection of PlayStation games crafted for the next generation.
> 
> 
> 
> 4. Welcome to a seamless gaming experience with our ultra-high speed SSD, where fast loading is just the beginning. Engage your senses with revolutionary haptic feedback, adaptive triggers, and 3D Audio technology, all while diving into an unparalleled array of new PlayStation games.
> 
> 
> 
> 5. Embrace the next level of gaming with our cutting-edge SSD, offering unmatched loading speeds. Feel every moment with precise haptic feedback, adaptive triggers, and immersive 3D Audio, and get exclusive access to a groundbreaking collection of new PlayStation games

No matter the project, it is helpful to have variations to choose from, especially when each one is unique from the other. You can also combine elements from multiple variations if none feels perfect.

Using this tip can be especially helpful if you experience writer’s block or just struggle with word or tone choice. The speed at which ChatGPT can generate alternatives will save countless hours at the thesaurus.

### Tips:

- Don’t be afraid to request multiple options.
- Asking for variations can help when you’re stuck or searching for the perfect wording.

### Conclusion

**Mix and match the above tips to see what works best for you and your editing style**. Keep changing phrases or words in your prompts until you get somewhere close to what you want. The results will probably be less than perfect, so be ready to edit. Human brains are still the best tools in the content writer’s tool kit, and they should always look over ChatGPT-generated work.
