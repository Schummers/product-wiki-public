## A Note on Interface Guidelines

After a couple of years, [are these guidelines still valid](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/durability-of-usability-guidelines/)? These usability guidelines are dependent mostly on human behavior, which changes slowly, and a lot less on the specifics of a given technology, which can change quickly. As you adopt the following guidelines in your own work, consider the core user behaviors and usability principles at play (not just the technology). Don’t throw out old findings because of their age.
